# Glimmerspark Industries

**Tags:** #Faction #WorldBuilding

---

## Overview
The technology company behind the prototype [[Sending Phone]] — emblem of the new industrial age flowing from [[Phandelver]]. Its name (and almost certainly its founder) traces back to [[Glimmerspark Tumpleton]], the gnome artificer the [[Wolfpack]] met in [[Leilon]] during [[Lost Mine of Phandelver|the previous age]].

Company motto, seen painted on its trucks: **"One World. One Voice."** ([[Session 14]]).

## Known History
- Prototype phones reached [[Wild Fury]] (via [[Halia]]) and [[Icarus]] (via his father, restricted) in [[Session 1]]; [[Blackjack]] promptly stole the latter.
- Runs **armored truck convoys** through the frozen wastes, bearing the company logo and motto ([[Session 14]]). One driver, [[Garvin]], is smuggling [[Icarus]] into [[Hartsvale]], believing him to be his lost brother Gradley.
- The convoy **reached [[Hartsvale]]** in [[Session 15]]; a company **ID pass** (plus a soot mustache and jacket) let [[Icarus]] slip past the city's loading-dock guards.
- **Gifted [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] an [[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** ([[Session 16]]) — the device he handed the party for capturing **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** alive. The company is not merely trading with [[Hartsvale]]; it is **equipping a frost giant lord's monster-hunt**, which puts Glimmerspark hardware at both ends of this arc: the [[Sending Phone|phones]] that started it and the collar meant to finish it.
