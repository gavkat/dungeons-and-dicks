# The Boner Bros

**Tags:** #Faction #Party

---

## Overview
The party. Founded as the bond between [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] ([[Session 2]]) over the wrestling hero "Bonecrusher," and grown into the collective name for the whole adventuring crew.

## Members
- [[Wild Fury]] — divine warrior; missing father; wyvern [[Dave]].
- [[Icarus]] — wild-magic noble; twice dead, currently a pink-haired half-orc; butler [[Gearsley]].
- [[Blackjack]] — thief turned **Shepherd** of [[The Great Devourer]]; bears the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]].
- [[Rik]] — pit champion seeking a cure for [[Marisya]]; wears the [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]] — which **unsealed the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]'s hidden stairway** ([[Session 18]]) — and wielded the [[Flame Tongue Whip]] until it was stolen ([[Session 17]]).

## Companions
- [[Gearsley]] — butler, driver, dead-raiser, heart-shooter.
- [[Dave]] — wyvern.
- [[Polly]] — teleporting parrot; calls Icarus "daddy."

## Known History
- Formed aboard [[The Tempest's Temptation]]; survived the [[Sovereign Prophet]], the [[Itju-Sha]], and the [[Tomb of Tzentak]].
- Trekked three days through the frozen wastes toward [[Hartsvale]] guided by the exiled Frost Giant [[Vlad]], harried nightly by the [[Baba Yaga]]'s nightmares ([[Session 14]]). Entry to the city secured by [[Wild Fury]]'s declaration as Vlad's champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]] — while a separated [[Icarus]] is smuggled in by [[Garvin]].
- Split at [[Hartsvale]]'s gates ([[Session 15]]): [[Blackjack]] and the warforged [[Gearsley]] were turned away to an eastern outpost, [[Icarus]] was captured by [[Kenky]], and [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fought the Krug trials — where Wild Fury's father was revealed as the champion **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**.
- **Wiped out in the arena and brought back** ([[Session 16]]): the Maiden's Champion cut down [[Icarus]], [[Rik]], and [[Wild Fury]], and only **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]'s secret intervention** saved them. Now bound by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain and marching on the **[[Spine of the World]]** to cage **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** — with **[[Gearsley]] returned** and **Wild Fury II** in the party, but **[[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] held hostage** in [[Hartsvale]].
- **Into the [[Spine of the World]]** ([[Session 17]]): fought off the [[Ice Golems|ice golems]] that had been the shadowy figures in the snow, banished a **colossal ice titan**, met the Feywild flumph [[Phlumphi]], and descended into the **[[Dragon-Skull Cave]]** — where a **gold illusion** nearly dissolved three of them in acid and an **unseen thief** robbed the camp of its gold, rations, one of [[Gearsley]]'s guns, and [[Rik]]'s [[Flame Tongue Whip]].- **Down a man and a mountain** ([[Session 18]]): below the mural they found the frozen goblin [[Archibald]], a [[Wand of Wonder]], an alchemical laboratory and the archive of **[[Vraxis]]** — whom they collared by mistake, believing him **[[The Devil in the Ice|the Devil in the Ice]]**. Vraxis **killed [[Icarus]]** and brought the cavern down; **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** caught the collapsing threshold on his back so his son could run, and was **sealed inside**. They ran for the threshold with [[Gearsley]]'s **[[Thoughts & Prayers|pistols]]** and nothing else they had been sent for.
