# Flame Tongue Whip

**Tags:** #Item #Weapon

---

## Overview
A flaming whip snatched from the altar of the [[Tomb of Tzentak]]'s trophy room by [[Blackjack]] and tossed to [[Rik]], who used it to carve through the tomb's mummies ([[Session 10]]). Rik's signature weapon since.

## Current Whereabouts
**Stolen.** During the party's extended rest in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 17]]), an **unseen thief** slipped into camp past [[Gearsley]]'s watch and took the whip **straight out of [[Rik]]'s holster**, along with the party's gold, all their rations, and one of Gearsley's guns. Nobody saw who — or what — took it.
