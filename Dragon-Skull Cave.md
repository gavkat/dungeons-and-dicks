# Dragon-Skull Cave

**Tags:** #Location

---

## Overview
The cave in the [[Spine of the World]] whose entrance is the **gaping jaws of an ancient dragon's skull**, set into a cliff-face — the destination of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]) and the supposed lair of **[[The Devil in the Ice]]**. The party crossed its threshold in [[Session 17]], serenaded by [[Rik]]'s bagpipes.

Inside, a thick **pale blue frost-fog** rolls across the floor, **deadening all sound** and carrying a pungent stench of **ancient ice and ozone**. The fossilised teeth of the dragon give way to massive stalactites and stalagmites as the passage descends.

## The Carvings
The cavern walls are covered in **massive carvings depicting a primeval war between giants and dragons**, leading to a terrifying **mural of a five-headed dragon matriarch**. At the center of that mural is an **ancient dragon rune** — which [[Rik]] recognized as an **exact match to the runes embossed into the palm of his [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]**.

The mural marks where the path **forks left and right** — and it is also a **door**. In [[Session 18]] [[Rik]] pressed the **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]** to the dragon mural and **unsealed a hidden marble stairway** down into the cave's lower levels.

- **Left corridor** — opens onto a shimmering chamber apparently filled with **mountains of gold**. It is an **illusion**: it ensnares those who see it with visions of boundless wealth and walks them into a pool of **suffocating, acidic sludge**. It took [[Gearsley]] and **both Wild Furys** ([[Session 17]]).
- **Right corridor** — an **old campsite**, overshadowed by a towering **nine-foot yeti petrified in solid stone** (beheaded by [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]). The party took an extended rest here — and were **robbed in their sleep** by [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]. The petrified yeti was the work of the goblin adventurer **[[Archibald]]**, who turned it to stone with a **stolen wand** ([[Session 18]]).

## Below the Mural
Behind the dragon mural, the **hidden marble stairway** descends into levels nobody in [[Hartsvale]] seems to have mentioned:

- **The petrified forest** — a frozen wood of **blue ice and basalt**. Beneath a **towering icicle** lay the frozen remains of the goblin **[[Archibald]]** and his letter. A **tentacled stalagmite monstrosity** lairs here, and ambushed the party's attempt to bury him. [[Yeti-Tykes|Yeti-tykes]] den in these levels.
- **The abandoned alchemical laboratory** — strange reagents, including an ambiguously labelled **"Liomund's Tiny Hut"** potion, a **fire-belching elixir**, and a **feathered draught** that turns the drinker into a **goose**. Its stores are volatile: **green alchemical fires detonated** through the collapse.
- **[[Vraxis]]'s archive** — the **lavish subterranean archive** of the cobalt dragon soldier, found by following the smell of **smoked sausages and Cajun spices**. Its **load-bearing pillars** were hacked through by a frenzied [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] in the fight that brought the whole cavern down.

## Session History

### Session 16
- Named as the party's destination in [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain; the party reached the cliff-face beneath the dragon skull and were met by a blizzard, an avalanching frost cloud, and shadowy figures.

### Session 17
- After fighting off the [[Ice Golems|ice golems]], the party stepped into the skull's jaws and descended through the frost-fog.
- Found the **giant-and-dragon war carvings** and the **five-headed dragon matriarch mural**, whose central rune [[Rik]] matched to his **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]**.
- Took the **left corridor** at [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]'s insistence and were caught by the **gold illusion** and its **acid pool**; the party hauled all three victims clear.
- Explored the **right corridor**, beheaded the **petrified yeti**, and rested at the old campsite — where an **unseen thief** stripped them of gold, rations, a gun, and the [[Flame Tongue Whip]].

### Session 18
- **The mural opened.** [[Rik]]'s [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]] against the dragon carving unsealed a **hidden marble stairway** into a **petrified forest of blue ice and basalt**.
- The frozen goblin **[[Archibald]]** was found beneath a towering icicle; a **tentacled stalagmite monstrosity** ambushed the burial, and [[Icarus]] was briefly **displaced into the astral plane** during the fight.
- Deeper still: the **abandoned alchemical laboratory**, and beyond it **[[Vraxis]]'s lavish subterranean archive**, tracked down by the smell of his cooking.
- **The cave was destroyed.** [[Vraxis]]'s **tempest of frost and lightning**, a **frenzied [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** hacking through **load-bearing pillars**, and **detonating green alchemical fires** brought on a **cataclysmic cave-in** — collapsing the **dragon-skull threshold** itself and sealing **Wild Fury II** inside under millions of tons of rock.
