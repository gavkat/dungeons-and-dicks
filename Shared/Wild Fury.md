# Wild Fury

**Tags:** #character #pc #Shared #Campaign1 #Campaign2 #BottomFeeders #BonerBros

> **Shared across campaigns.** A player character in *both* [[Lost Mine of Phandelver]] (Campaign 1) and [[Echoes of Phandelver]] (Campaign 2) — the same Tabaxi paladin, five years on.

## Description
**Wild Fury III** — a Tabaxi paladin and heir to the [[Lionshield Coster]], the covert guild that quietly keeps Faerûn's political order. A divine warrior who channels the power of his ancestors, bonded to his wyvern [[Dave]], and forever chasing the shadow of his missing father.

## In Campaign 1 — Lost Mine of Phandelver
First found locked in a prison cell in [[Cragmaw Castle]], "Wild Fury III" was freed by the [[Wolfpack]] and joined their crusade against the Black Spider.

- Empowered by an amulet against the vampire [[Lightning Steel]]; inherited the sword [[Lightbringer]] from [[Frederick Iron-Thunder|Frederick]] in exchange for a giant Elk steed (Session 11).
- In the trials of [[Old Owl Well]], his test — *the Roar of Pride* — forced a confrontation with his father (Session 17). He was then poisoned by the shapeshifter [[Talice]] wearing [[Halia]]'s face.
- At [[Wyvern Tor]], a vision of his **grandfather** revealed the origins of the [[Lionshield Coster]]. There he recovered his grandfather's armor and sword — and a single **wyvern egg**, which he vowed to protect (Session 20). That egg hatched into [[Dave]].
- His blood was among the three the Black Spider harvested to mirror the Ilefarn ritual at the [[Wave Echo Cave|Forge of Spells]] (Session 25).
- **Epilogue:** with his father absent, Wild Fury III assumed leadership of the [[Lionshield Coster]] and raised [[Dave]]. Then [[Halia]] delivered the revelation that shattered his peace — *his father was still alive.*

## In Campaign 2 — Echoes of Phandelver
Five years later, Wild Fury is one of the [[Boner Bros]], still haunted and still searching.

**Core traits:**
- Channels devastating divine smites ([[Session 10]], [[Session 12]]) and spells like Command and Suggestion.
- Still bonded to [[Dave]].
- Suffers panic attacks rooted in his failure to save civilians from the dragon that destroyed [[Leilon]] five years ago — the very dragon ([[Switchblade]]) the Wolfpack faced in Campaign 1.

**Personal quest:**
- Find his missing father, last seen near the [[Ice Spires]] — a lead from [[Halia]] ([[Session 1]]), and the same "father is alive" thread left dangling at the end of Campaign 1. Also framed as hunting a "missing guildmate" ([[Session 5]]) of the Lionshield Coster. The mysterious arena flyer ([[The White Claw]], [[Session 12]]) points toward his long-lost father — the thread now driving the push to [[Hartsvale]] ([[Session 14]]). **Found ([[Session 15]]):** his father is **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — **Wild Fury II** — [[Hartsvale]]'s Maiden's Champion. **Reunion, of a kind ([[Session 16]]):** his father fought him with **no memory of him**, cut him down, and told him *"You always disappointed me, son."* **Liberated** under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain, Wild Fury II now travels with the party — and has finally explained the six years he was gone.
- Carries a prototype [[Sending Phone]] from [[Halia]], who told him of [[The Tethered]].

### Campaign 2 Session History
- **[[Session 1]]** — Met [[Halia]]; learned of [[The Tethered]]; received the [[Sending Phone]] and the [[Ice Spires]] lead.
- **[[Session 2]]** — Fleeing [[Leilon]] triggered the dragon-attack trauma. Bonded with [[Rik]]; formed the [[Boner Bros]].
- **[[Session 3]]** — With [[Blackjack]], found [[Rik]] hanged and fought the dominated [[Sauriv]].
- **[[Session 4]]** — Escaped the doomed [[The Tempest's Temptation]] on [[Dave]]'s back.
- **[[Session 5]]** — Refused to surrender [[Dave]] to the [[Mage Guard]]; shared his hunt for the missing guildmate.
- **[[Session 7]]** — Cast Command in the fighting pit to help [[Rik]] win.
- **[[Session 8]]** — Cleared the [[Itju-Sha]] escape with a Suggestion.
- **[[Session 10]]** — Smote the mummies of the [[Tomb of Tzentak]].
- **[[Session 11]]** — A final divine stand against [[Tzentak]]; polymorphed into a Giant Ape by [[Icarus]].
- **[[Session 12]]** — Reunited with [[Glenn]], his old guild's Head of HR; destroyed Krampus ([[Agatha the Hag]]); received the [[The White Claw]] flyer.
- **[[Session 13]]** — Scouted ahead on [[Dave]] during the trek north to [[Hartsvale]]; lost [[Dave]] to [[The Ashen Figure]] when a [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black" was called.
- **[[Session 14]]** — Mercy-killed the party's mauled polar bear and custom-fashioned its fur into a "dope" hooded jacket (making [[Vlad]] violently vomit). Declared **[[Vlad]]'s champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]]** — the [[The White Claw]] arena itself, and the party's way into [[Hartsvale]] — as the flyer pointing toward his long-lost father drove the trek onward.
- **[[Session 15]]** — Entered [[Hartsvale]] with [[Vlad]] and [[Rik]] and fought the **Krug combat trials** alongside the [[Krug Gladiators]]. After [[Icarus]] banished the arena's anti-magic snail, Wild Fury hyped [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and the crowd to make the banishment permanent. The king then summoned the Maiden's Champion, **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — and Wild Fury **recognized him as his own long-lost father.** Per [[Vlad]]'s loophole, defeating his father would void Vlad's exile without trapping Wild Fury as the replacement Maiden's Champion.
- **[[Session 16]]** — **Lost the fight, and worse.** Watching [[Rik]] beat his father to the edge of death, Wild Fury III **threw himself into the blow** to save him — and the sacrifice **failed to break the dark spell**. His father struck [[Rik]] down, **tore away Wild Fury's shield**, said ***"You always disappointed me, son,"*** and buried a great axe in his chest; he collapsed into a pool of his own blood. **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] secretly saved the party from death**, and they woke in an infirmary after a shared [[Baba Yaga]] nightmare. In the throne room he saw his father burst in **lucid and furious** — [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] had **manipulated** Wild Fury II into the fight. Under Kholodan's bargain his father was **liberated**, and on the three-day trek to the [[Spine of the World]] finally told him where he had been: [[Dew Dew Bun]], the [[Elven Lute]], and the naming of all of [[The Dark Six]].
