# Dew-Dew Bun

**Tags:** #character #npc #pc #Shared #Campaign1 #Campaign2 #Mystery

> **Shared across campaigns.** A prequel-era member of the [[Wolfpack]] in [[Lost Mine of Phandelver]] (Campaign 1), whose disappearance, six years before [[Session 16]], set off the investigation behind [[Echoes of Phandelver]] (Campaign 2).

## Description
**Fairy/Bard.** A celebrity fairy bard of great renown, whose music carried her fame across oceans, lands and planes. The source of her power was far older than she was: an **[[Elven Lute|ancient elven lute]]**, originally a gift from **[[Tymora]]** to her sister **[[Beshaba]]**. **Status: Whereabouts unknown.**

## In Campaign 1 — Lost Mine of Phandelver
A prequel-era member of the [[Wolfpack]], shadowed everywhere by her bodyguard [[Switchblade]].

Dew-Dew Bun's performance at the Daggerford fair was the spark that drew the future Wolfpack together — interrupted first by a warning-bleating sheep ([[Finethir Shinebright|Shinebright]] in disguise) and then by [[Tlaloc Nightfoot|Tlaloc]]'s beastly entourage crashing the crowd. Her fame also made her the obsession of [[Arnolt Noke|Noke]], whose mania over her drove him to summon zombies clad in her merchandise. When the prequel ended and [[Finethir Shinebright|Shinebright]] silenced her with a spell and froze the party in time, Dew-Dew Bun's thread was cut.

- **[[Prequel - The Wild Sheep's Chase|Prequel: The Wild Sheep's Chase]]** — Performed at the Daggerford fair under [[Switchblade]]'s protection; the unwitting object of [[Arnolt Noke|Noke]]'s obsession. Silenced by [[Finethir Shinebright|Shinebright]] as he froze the party in time; her fate thereafter is unknown.

## In Campaign 2 — Echoes of Phandelver
She is the **first domino**. She **vanished without a trace six years ago** ([[Session 16]]), and her fame was real enough that her disappearance registered as a matter for the [[Lionshield Coster]]. It is what sent **Wild Fury II** — **[[The White Claw (Champion)|"The White Claw"]]**, then head of the [[Lionshield Coster]] — out to investigate. That investigation traced her powers to the [[Elven Lute]], the lute to the sisters [[Tymora]] and [[Beshaba]], and the sisters to the seven leaders of [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] — uncovering that the six who remained became **[[The Dark Six]]**. Tracking those entities brought Wild Fury II to [[Hartsvale]] five years ago, where a supernatural rage trapped him.

In other words: the hunt for a missing fairy bard is the reason [[Wild Fury]] grew up without a father, and the reason the party is in the frozen north at all.

Whether Dew-Dew Bun is alive, who took her, and where the lute is now are all **unanswered**.

### Campaign 2 Session History
