# Thoughts & Prayers

**Tags:** #Item #Weapon

---

## Overview
**[[Gearsley]]'s beloved twin pistols**, named in [[Session 18]]: **"Thoughts"** and **"Prayers."** The butler has been quietly lethal with a gun since [[Session 1]] — the Naga [[Ouraeus]], [[Tzentak]]'s rotten heart — but the pair were only named once one of them had been **stolen and won back**.

## Current Whereabouts
**Reunited.** One of the pair was taken by the **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]** that raided the party's camp in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 17]]). In [[Session 18]] the tyke holding it **shot itself** in a panic when a goose-shaped [[Icarus]] flared his wings and honked at it, returning the pistol to [[Gearsley]].

## Session History

### Session 17
- One of the two was **stolen** from the party's camp by the then-unseen thief.

### Session 18
- **Recovered** in [[Vraxis]]'s subterranean archive when the [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tyke]] holding it panicked at goose-[[Icarus]] and **shot itself** — **reuniting the gunslinger with both pistols**.
