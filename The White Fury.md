# The White Fury

**Tags:** #Lore #Mystery #Campaign2

> Not to be confused with **[[Wild Fury]]**, the party's Tabaxi paladin — though he has watched it take his father.

---

## Overview
A **feral, supernatural rage** that has run through [[Hartsvale]] since at least five years ago. It is **not a temper**: it takes people over, wipes their judgment, and — as of [[Session 20]] — **spreads**.

- **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** — overwhelmed by a **supernatural, blinding rage** on arriving in Hartsvale five years ago, which stopped him leaving and let [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] aim him at his own son ([[Session 16]]). It **surged again** under the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] and was broken by an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** ([[Session 18]]).
- **[[Vlad]]** — released from a ceiling cage by Kholodan with **the same feral white fury that had claimed Wild Fury II** in his eyes ([[Session 19]]). **Kholodan's death freed him** of it ([[Session 20]]).
- **[[Hartsvale]]'s revolutionary mob** — moments after Kholodan fell, their **own eyes were overtaken by the infectious white fury**, and they turned on **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]** ([[Session 20]]).

## Where it came from
[[Session 20]]'s nightmare visions showed Kholodan, **weeping over his wife dying in childbirth**, **bargaining to a falling star and [[The Dark Six]] to birth an avatar of fury**. That avatar was Zarya — in truth the **[[Baba Yaga]]**, a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]** — whose **obsidian-star phylactery** the party shattered at dawn, **breaking the God's tether** as the blizzard began to thaw.

The source never states outright that the white fury *is* the avatar's power, nor which of the Six it served. **The Fury** of [[The Dark Six]] is the obvious reading, and the vault treats it as an inference only. Whether the fury lifted from the mob when the star broke is not stated.

## Session History

### Session 16
- Revealed (unnamed) as the **supernatural rage** that trapped **Wild Fury II** in [[Hartsvale]] and that [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] exploited to set him on his son.

- Returned in Wild Fury II as **supernatural bloodlust** deep under the mountain, far from Hartsvale; broken by the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**.

### Session 19
- First called **white fury**: [[Vlad]] burst from Kholodan's ceiling cage with **the same feral white fury that had claimed Wild Fury II**.

### Session 20
- **Released [[Vlad]]** when [[Rik]] beheaded Kholodan.
- **Spread to the mob**, who turned on [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] — "infectious," per the recap.
- Traced in vision to Kholodan's **bargain with a falling star and [[The Dark Six]]** for **an avatar of fury**, whose phylactery the party destroyed.
