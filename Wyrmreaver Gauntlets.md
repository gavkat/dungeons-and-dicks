# Wyrmreaver Gauntlets

**Tags:** #Item

---

## Overview
[[Rik]]'s gauntlets, first named in [[Session 17]]. **Ancient dragon runes are embossed into the palm** of them — a detail that sat unremarked until the party reached the **mural of the five-headed dragon matriarch** deep inside the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]].

After numerous failed attempts and strained recollections, [[Rik]] pieced it together: the **ancient dragon rune etched into the center of the stone mural is an exact match** to the runes in his gauntlets' palm.

Nothing has been said about where Rik got them, what the rune means, or what the match implies — only that the gauntlets and a monument to a primeval war between giants and dragons are marked with the same sign.
