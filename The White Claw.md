## Overview
An arena "where hearts fail." A promotional flyer reached [[Wild Fury]] at [[Holly Hollow]] ([[Session 12]]) — a deadly invitation to face a maiden's champion.

The flyer **points toward [[Wild Fury]]'s long-lost father**, and drove the party's trek to [[Hartsvale]] ([[Session 14]]). Its relation to [[The Krug]] — and whether the "maiden" is Hartsvale's Twilight Maiden, [[Princess Zaria]] — is unconfirmed.
