# The White Claw

**Tags:** #Location #Hook

> This page is the **arena** (the Krug). For the Maiden's Champion who bears the same name — [[Wild Fury]]'s father — see **[[The White Claw (Champion)]]**.

---

## Overview
An arena "where hearts fail," in [[Hartsvale]] — known there as **"The Krug."** A promotional flyer reached [[Wild Fury]] at [[Holly Hollow]] ([[Session 12]]): a deadly invitation to face a maiden's champion. The "maiden" is Hartsvale's Twilight Maiden, **[[Princess Zaria]]**.

The flyer **points toward [[Wild Fury]]'s long-lost father** — and in [[Session 15]] that pointer paid off: the maiden's champion, introduced by the name **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**, *is* Wild Fury's father. The flyer drove the party's trek to [[Hartsvale]] ([[Session 14]]); to get the party into the city, [[Vlad]] — the exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler — was persuaded to **declare Wild Fury III as his champion at the Krug**.

## Session History

### Session 12
- The promotional flyer reached [[Wild Fury]] at [[Holly Hollow]]: face a maiden's champion in an arena where hearts fail.

### Session 14
- The flyer's pointer toward [[Wild Fury]]'s father drove the three-day trek to [[Hartsvale]].
- [[Vlad]] declared [[Wild Fury]] III his champion at the Krug, giving the party their way into the city.

### Session 15
- [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fought the Krug **combat trials** before [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]: three waves of tigers, a crag cat and four-armed gorillon, and an **anti-magic snail** — the last banished by an unrecognized [[Icarus]] (turned into a potted plant by wild magic).
- The Maiden's Champion was summoned and named **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — revealed to be [[Wild Fury]]'s **father**. Per [[Vlad]]'s loophole, defeating him would void Vlad's exile without binding Wild Fury as the replacement champion.

_The father–son bout is set but **not yet fought.**_
