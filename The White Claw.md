# The White Claw

**Tags:** #Location #Hook

---

## Overview
An arena "where hearts fail," in [[Hartsvale]] — known there as **"The Krug."** A promotional flyer reached [[Wild Fury]] at [[Holly Hollow]] ([[Session 12]]): a deadly invitation to face a maiden's champion. The "maiden" is Hartsvale's Twilight Maiden, **[[Princess Zaria]]**.

The flyer **points toward [[Wild Fury]]'s long-lost father**, and drove the party's trek to [[Hartsvale]] ([[Session 14]]). To get the party into the city, [[Vlad]] — the exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler — was persuaded to **declare Wild Fury III as his champion at the Krug**.
