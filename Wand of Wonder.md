# Wand of Wonder

**Tags:** #Item

---

## Overview
A **crooked wand** recovered from the frozen body of the goblin adventurer **[[Archibald]]** beneath the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]], and **attuned by [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]** ([[Session 18]]). [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] recognized it as a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard** — the closest the son has come to a tender moment with his father.

Its **first test**, aimed at a fleeing [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tyke]], came out **inadvertently** as a **gale that splattered the creature against the cavern wall** — a wand that does what it likes, in the hands of a paladin.

[[Archibald]]'s letter says he **petrified the campsite's yeti with a stolen wand**; whether that wand and this one are the same is never stated.
