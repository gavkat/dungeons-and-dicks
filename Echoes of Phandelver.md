## The Premise

Five years have passed since the battle at the Forge of Spells, where the [[Wolfpack]] defeated the Black Spider (chronicled in [[Lost Mine of Phandelver]]). A new age of technology, industrialization, and prosperity has dawned — warforged and common folk building side by side, powered by the rediscovered mines of [[Phandelver]] and the patronage of "Prometheus," secretly [[Mordenkainen]].

But beneath the surface: rumors, whispers — people changing, eyes turning black, [[The Tethered]] falling ill since the Forge reawakened. *The Wolfpack saved Phandelver — but at what cost?*

## The Story So Far

- **Act I — The Sea** ([[Session 1]]–[[Session 4]]): Recruited into the [[Bottom Feeders]] at [[Leilon]]; ghost ships in [[The Choke]]; murders aboard [[The Tempest's Temptation]]; the [[Sovereign Prophet]] revealed and [[Captain Siren Shellsword]] devoured.
- **Interlude — Castaways** ([[Session 5]]–[[Session 6]]): The [[Mage Guard]], the island of [[Mintan]], [[Koa]]'s revelations, the Prophet's ultimatum — and being swallowed whole.
- **Act II — The Desert** ([[Session 7]]–[[Session 11]]): Captured by the [[Itju-Sha]]; pit fights and a Fury-Road escape; [[Blackjack]] reborn as Shepherd; the [[Tomb of Tzentak]]; [[Ouraeus]]'s betrayal and [[Tzentak]]'s destruction.
- **Act III — The Frost** ([[Session 12]]–): The desert replaced by tundra; [[Holly Hollow]]'s Mid-Winter Jubilee; [[Agatha the Hag]] slain; [[Glenn]] crowned Father Klaus; the [[The White Claw]] flyer arrives. [[Ilbryn Darksight|Ilbryn]] reveals [[Tzentak]] was a [[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]] and that five more remain; the trek north to [[Hartsvale]] costs the party [[Dave]], taken by a [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black"; the Frost Giant [[Vlad]] appears. Vlad — exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler — guides the party to the city through the [[Baba Yaga]]'s nightmares, naming [[Wild Fury]] his champion for [[The White Claw|The Krug]] (the White Claw arena itself); a separated [[Icarus]] survives the dragon soldier [[Vraxis]] and is smuggled toward Hartsvale by [[Garvin]], who believes Icarus is his lost brother Gradley. At [[Hartsvale]] the party splits ([[Session 15]]): [[Blackjack]] and the warforged [[Gearsley]] are barred at the gates over [[Phandelver's Pact]]; [[Icarus]] — his true form an **Aarakocra**, his father named as [[Reginald]] — is trapped by his Kenku childhood friend [[Kenky]]; and [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fight the Krug trials, where the Maiden's Champion **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** is revealed to be **Wild Fury's own father**. The bout goes badly ([[Session 16]]): spellbound and with no memory of his son, **Wild Fury II** kills [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey]], drops [[Icarus]] and [[Rik]], and axes [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] through the chest — *"You always disappointed me, son."* **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] secretly saves the party**, and [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] — who had **manipulated Wild Fury II into the fight** and quietly **captured [[Gearsley]] and [[Blackjack]]** — trades their freedom, [[Vlad]]'s life, and Wild Fury II's release for one errand: cage **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** in the [[Spine of the World]], with [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] left as collateral. On the trek, Wild Fury II finally explains the missing years — the vanished bard [[Dew Dew Bun]], the [[Elven Lute]] that [[Tymora]] gave [[Beshaba]], the **seven** [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] leaders, and the naming of all of **[[The Dark Six]]** — before a blizzard and **shadowy figures** meet them at the dragon-skull cave.
