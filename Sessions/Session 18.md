# Session 18: Devil in the Ice

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** [[Rik]]'s **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]** unseal a hidden stairway below the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]; the frozen goblin **[[Archibald]]** turns out to have petrified the campsite yeti and to have been hunting the **[[The Devil in the Ice|Devil in the Ice]]** to cure his brothers; [[Icarus]] is displaced into the astral plane and told **"Fly, boy. Fly."** by [[The Cloaked Figure|a cloaked figure]]; [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] attunes to a **[[Wand of Wonder]]** and immediately kills a [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tyke]] with it; the camp thieves are unmasked as **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]** and [[Gearsley]] gets **[[Thoughts & Prayers|"Thoughts" and "Prayers"]]** back; the party collars the wrong monster, and **[[Vraxis]]** kills [[Icarus]] and brings the mountain down on **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]**.

## Summary

After discovering their **plundered camp**, [[Rik]] pressed his **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]** to the cavern's **dragon mural**, unsealing a **hidden marble stairway** that descended into a **petrified forest of blue ice and basalt**. Beneath a towering icicle the party uncovered the **frozen remains of the goblin adventurer [[Archibald]]**, and a **letter to his brother [[Archibald|Icey]]**. The letter answered a question the party had left behind upstairs: **Archibald had petrified the campsite's yeti with a stolen wand**, while hunting **"[[The Devil in the Ice|the Devil in the Ice]]"** in the hope of **curing his brothers' afflictions**.

Their attempt to bury him was ambushed by a **tentacled stalagmite monstrosity**. During the fight [[Icarus]] was **briefly displaced into the starry astral plane**, where an **[[The Cloaked Figure|enigmatic cloaked figure]]** cryptically **warned him of his reckless chaos** before commanding: ***"Fly, boy. Fly."*** In the aftermath, [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **attuned to Archibald's crooked [[Wand of Wonder]]** and shared a **tender moment with his father** — [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] recognized the wand as a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard**. Moments later [[Rik]] caught a **thieving juvenile [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tyke]]** and **smacked it with an offered fish**; [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] tested his new wand and **inadvertently unleashed a gale that splattered the fleeing tyke against the cavern wall**.

Venturing deeper, the party entered an **abandoned alchemical laboratory** stocked with strange reagents — among them an ambiguously labelled **"Liomund's Tiny Hut"** potion. [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] recklessly **downed an elixir that made him belch fire**, while [[Icarus]] drank a **feathered draught that temporarily transformed him into a goose**. Lured onward by **sizzling smoked sausages and Cajun spices**, they breached the **lavish subterranean archive of [[Vraxis]]**, a **cobalt draconic scholar**. Goose-[[Icarus]] **flared his wings and honked aggressively** at the surviving **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]** playing with the party's stolen gear, panicking the one holding **[[Gearsley]]'s firearm into shooting itself** — reuniting the gunslinger with his beloved twin pistols, **[[Thoughts & Prayers|"Thoughts" and "Prayers"]]**.

As [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] groaned under a **surge of supernatural bloodlust**, the party attempted to ambush [[Vraxis]] with the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar|arcane dampener collar]]**, convinced he was **"the devil in the ice," aka "[[Baba Yaga]]."** [[Vraxis]] **scoffed at the insult**, declaring that the mountain **merely echoed nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle**, and unleashed a **tempest of frost and lightning**. A **frenzied [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** attacked the dragon and **inadvertently hacked through load-bearing pillars**, fracturing the cavern. Desperate to turn the tide, [[Icarus]] **bargained with [[Vraxis]], claiming he was an immortal god**; amused, [[Vraxis]] **rocketed the sorcerer through the ceiling**, **stopped his heart with a lethal jolt of lightning**, and **hurled his limp body into the abyss**.

[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] seized the chaotic distraction to **snap the dampener collar onto his father**, **breaking the bloodlust** just as the cavern suffered a **cataclysmic cave-in**. Racing through **detonating green alchemical fires** and falling stone, the party neared the **dragon-skull threshold** when a **massive granite monolith sheared loose** above [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]. In a **blur of white fur**, [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] **tackled his son into the snow**, **catching the crushing mountain across his own back**. Bracing the **collapsing stone jaws with his bare paws**, the elder warrior **ordered his son to run**, declaring that **after fighting for other men, he would die on his own terms**. Desperate to save him, [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **lunged with the [[Immovable Rod]]** — but his **footing gave way**. The button clicked, and the rod **locked rigidly into empty air inches short of the stone**, hovering uselessly in the blizzard as **millions of tons of rock crashed down, sealing his father within the dark**.

## Key Developments

- **The [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] has a lower level.** [[Rik]]'s **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]** pressed to the **dragon mural** unsealed a **hidden marble stairway** into a **petrified forest of blue ice and basalt** — the [[Session 17]] rune match was a **key**, not just a coincidence.
- **The petrified yeti is explained.** [[Archibald]], a **goblin adventurer** frozen beneath a towering icicle, **petrified the campsite's yeti with a stolen wand** — and was himself **hunting "[[The Devil in the Ice|the Devil in the Ice]]"**, to **cure his brothers' afflictions**. His **letter to his brother Icey** is how the party knows.
- **[[Icarus]] was displaced into the astral plane** mid-fight with a **tentacled stalagmite monstrosity**, and met an **[[The Cloaked Figure|enigmatic cloaked figure]]** who **warned him of his reckless chaos** and told him ***"Fly, boy. Fly."***
- **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] attuned to a [[Wand of Wonder]]** — [[Archibald]]'s crooked wand, which his father recognized as a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard**. Its first test **splattered a fleeing [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tyke]] against the cavern wall**.
- **The [[Session 17]] thieves were [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]** — juvenile yetis, caught **playing with the party's stolen gear**. [[Rik]] smacked one with an offered fish and [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]'s wand killed it as it fled; another **shot itself** with [[Gearsley]]'s gun when goose-[[Icarus]] honked at it.
- **[[Gearsley]]'s pistols have names:** **[[Thoughts & Prayers|"Thoughts" and "Prayers"]]** — the stolen one **recovered** from the tykes, reuniting him with the pair.
- **The cave holds an abandoned alchemical laboratory** (reagents including a **"Liomund's Tiny Hut"** potion) — a **fire-belching elixir** for [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] and a **feathered draught** that turned [[Icarus]] into a **goose**.
- **[[Vraxis]] is back, and he has an archive here.** The **cobalt dragon soldier** of [[Session 14]] keeps a **lavish subterranean archive** in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] — and cooks; the party found it by following **smoked sausages and Cajun spices**.
- **The party collared the wrong monster.** Convinced [[Vraxis]] was **"the devil in the ice," aka the [[Baba Yaga]]**, they ambushed him with the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** — and he **scoffed at the insult**.
- **The nightmares are Hartsvale's, not the mountain's.** [[Vraxis]] declared the mountain **merely echoes nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle** — directly contradicting Kholodan's [[Session 16]] story.
- **The bloodlust took [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] again**, and he **hacked through the cavern's load-bearing pillars** mid-frenzy, fracturing the chamber. **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] snapped the dampener collar onto his own father** and **broke it** — the collar spent on the wrong target, and the right one.
- **[[Vraxis]] killed [[Icarus]].** After the sorcerer **claimed to be an immortal god**, Vraxis **rocketed him through the ceiling**, **stopped his heart with lightning**, and **hurled his body into the abyss**. What became of him afterwards is not known.
- **The mountain came down.** Racing out through **detonating green alchemical fires**, [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] **tackled his son clear of a falling granite monolith** and **caught the mountain on his back**, bracing the stone jaws bare-pawed and **ordering his son to run** — *after fighting for other men, he would die on his own terms*.
- **The [[Immovable Rod]] failed.** [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] lunged with it, **lost his footing**, and the rod **locked into empty air inches short of the stone** — leaving his father **sealed in the dark** under millions of tons of rock.
