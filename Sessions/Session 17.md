# Session 17: The Spine of the World

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** The shadowy figures in the snow turn out to be **[[Ice Golems|ice golems]]**; [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] cannot earn his father's approval no matter what he kills; [[Icarus]]'s chaotic magic coughs up **[[Phlumphi]]**, a Feywild flumph who calls him an immortal god and hurries off to report that he and [[Polly]] have found each other; [[Rik]]'s **[[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]** match the dragon rune on a mural deep inside the **[[Dragon-Skull Cave]]**; an illusion of gold nearly dissolves three of the party in acid; and an unseen thief strips the camp of its gold, rations, one of [[Gearsley]]'s guns, and the [[Flame Tongue Whip]].

## Summary

An **avalanche of ice and snow** crashed down the treacherous mountain slopes and the shadowy figures from the cliff-face resolved into **[[Ice Golems|ice golems]]**, ambushing the party and forcing them into a desperate uphill battle through blinding arctic winds. Amidst the chaos an **awkward tension simmered between [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] and [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]**: the son repeatedly sought his distant father's validation while the battle-hardened veteran effortlessly outpaced him, leaving the younger warrior grappling with their lingering emotional disconnect. The skirmish escalated when a **colossal [[Ice Golems|ice titan]] erupted from the frost** — [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] struck it with a **crackling lightning javelin**, and [[Icarus]] **banished the behemoth to another plane of existence**.

Surging forward to the mountain crest, [[Icarus]]'s chaotic magic accidentally manifested **[[Phlumphi]]**, a friendly, floating **flumph from the Feywild**. The creature cheerfully proclaimed that the **legendary Icarus is celebrated across the fae realm as an immortal god** — then, spotting **[[Polly]]** perched on the sorcerer's shoulder, noted that **the two had successfully found each other**, announced that the news **had to be reported back**, and promptly faded out of existence. Standing at the precipice overlooking the **[[Spine of the World]]**, the party looked out at the distant speck of **[[Hartsvale]]** before stepping into the gaping jaws of the **[[Dragon-Skull Cave|dragon-skull cave]]**, serenaded by [[Rik]]'s bagpipes.

Descending into the fossilized depths, a thick **pale blue frost-fog** rolled over their boots, deadening all sound and carrying a pungent stench of ancient ice and ozone, while the teeth of the dragon slowly turned into massive stalactites and stalagmites. The cavern walls revealed **massive carvings of a primeval war between giants and dragons**, leading to a terrifying **mural of a five-headed dragon matriarch** that split the path into left and right passages. After numerous failed attempts and strained recollections, **[[Rik]] finally pieced the mystery together**: the **ancient dragon rune** etched into the center of the stone mural was an **exact match to the runes embossed into the palm of his [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]]**.

When the elder tabaxi dismissed his son's attempts to establish team hierarchy and stubbornly took the lead down the **left corridor**, the group stumbled upon what appeared to be a shimmering chamber filled with **mountains of gold**. The illusion ensnared **[[Gearsley]] and both Wild Furys** with visions of boundless wealth — prompting the father to bluntly snap at his son's warnings — as all three waded directly into a pool of **suffocating, acidic sludge**. In a frantic team effort the party worked collectively to snap their entranced companions out of the hallucinations and haul everyone free of the caustic mire before they could dissolve.

Retreating to explore the **right corridor**, the party discovered an **old campsite** overshadowed by a towering **nine-foot yeti petrified in solid stone**. To neutralize the threat and secure the perimeter, [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] **hacked the petrified monster's head clean off**, allowing the battered group to settle down for an extended rest under [[Gearsley]]'s alert watch. Their relief turned to dismay upon waking: during the night an **unseen thief** had slipped into camp undetected, **plundering their gold**, **taking every scrap of their rations**, **swiping one of [[Gearsley]]'s guns**, and **stealing [[Rik]]'s prized [[Flame Tongue Whip]] straight from his holster**.

## Key Developments

- **The [[Session 16]] cliffhanger answered:** the shadowy figures charging through the snow were **[[Ice Golems|ice golems]]**, and the avalanche was the opening of an uphill ambush.
- **A colossal [[Ice Golems|ice titan]] was banished, not killed.** [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] hit it with a **crackling lightning javelin**; [[Icarus]] sent it **to another plane of existence** — it is still out there somewhere.
- **The father-son rift is emotional, not magical.** Free of the rage, [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] still outpaces, dismisses and snaps at his son — who keeps chasing his validation and keeps failing to get it.
- **[[Phlumphi]], a Feywild flumph**, was accidentally manifested by [[Icarus]]'s chaotic magic. It claimed the **fae realm celebrates Icarus as an immortal god**, recognized **[[Polly]]** on his shoulder, said **"the two had found each other"** — and left to **report the news back**.
- **[[Rik]]'s [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]] are keyed to this place:** the runes embossed in their palm are an **exact match** to the ancient dragon rune at the center of the mural of the **five-headed dragon matriarch**.
- **The [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] is a record of a giant–dragon war** — frost-fog, fossil teeth turned to stalactites, carvings of a primeval war between giants and dragons, and a mural that forks the path.
- **The left corridor is a trap:** an illusion of **mountains of gold** ensnared [[Gearsley]] and **both Wild Furys** and walked them into a pool of **acidic sludge**. The party pulled all three out before they dissolved.
- **The right corridor holds an old campsite** and a **nine-foot yeti petrified in stone** — decapitated by [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] before the party rested there.
- **Robbed in their sleep.** An **unseen thief** got past [[Gearsley]]'s watch and took the party's **gold**, **all their rations**, **one of Gearsley's guns**, and **[[Rik]]'s [[Flame Tongue Whip]]**. Nobody saw it.
