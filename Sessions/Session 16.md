# Session 16: Maiden's Champion

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — [[Wild Fury]]'s father, **Wild Fury II** — butchers [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey]], drops [[Icarus]] and [[Rik]], and cuts down his own son with *"You always disappointed me, son"*; [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] secretly saves the party from death; [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] buys their freedom with a hunt for **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** in the [[Spine of the World]]; and Wild Fury II finally explains his disappearance — a vanished fairy bard, an [[Elven Lute|ancient elven lute]], and the naming of all six of [[The Dark Six]].

## Summary

The battle inside the [[The White Claw|Krug]] arena reached a heart-wrenching climax. **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — aka **Wild Fury II** — shrouded in bloodlust and with **no memory of his son**, wreaked havoc on the party, brutally **decapitating [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey the Minotaur]]**. In a desperate bid to turn the tide, [[Icarus]] transformed into a **Giant Ape** to wrestle with and jump on the berserker, and later unleashed a devastating burst of magical energy — though he was ultimately **knocked unconscious** by a brutal retaliatory blow. Pushed to the brink, [[Rik]] executed a furious counteroffensive that left the Maiden's Champion battered and staggering on the edge of death.

Realizing Rik's next strike would prove fatal, **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] leapt directly into harm's way**, taking Rik's blow himself to shield his father. The heroic sacrifice failed to break the dark spell. [[The White Claw (Champion)|White Claw]] immediately turned his rage onto [[Rik]] and **struck him down**, then turned back to his son, **ripped away his shield**, uttered the soul-crushing words — ***"You always disappointed me, son"*** — and brought his great axe down in a crushing strike that **concaved his chest**. Wild Fury III collapsed into a pool of his own blood as the roaring arena crowd faded into pitch blackness.

After surviving a harrowing **shared nightmare of the [[Baba Yaga]]**, the party awoke in an infirmary to discover that **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] had secretly saved them from impending death**. She escorted them to the grand throne room, where [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] sat flanked by **gnoll honor guards** and the exiled **[[Vlad]]**, muzzled and chained. Their audience was interrupted as **Wild Fury II burst through the doors**, furious at the frost giant lord for **manipulating him into attacking his own son**.

Capitalizing on their survival, [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] offered a bargain: in exchange for the party's **freedom**, the **voiding of [[Vlad]]'s execution**, and the **liberation of Wild Fury II**, they must venture into the **[[Spine of the World]]**, enter a **dragon-skull cave**, and capture **"[[The Devil in the Ice]]" alive** — which he claimed to be the source of the nightmares plaguing [[Hartsvale]] — using an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar|arcane dampener collar]]** gifted by [[Glimmerspark Industries]]. Kholodan then revealed he had **captured [[Gearsley]] and [[Blackjack]]**, but was persuaded to release Gearsley to join the expedition alongside Wild Fury II, while **[[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] were kept behind as collateral**.

During their three-day trek across the arctic tundra, **Wild Fury II finally revealed the truth behind his disappearance.** Six years prior, the celebrity fairy bard **[[Dew Dew Bun]]** had vanished without a trace, prompting him to investigate as **head of the [[Lionshield Coster]]**. His search uncovered the source of the fairy's powers: an **[[Elven Lute|ancient elven lute]]** that was originally a **gift from [[Tymora]] to her sister [[Beshaba]]** — two of the **seven [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] leaders**. Detailing the tumultuous sisterly relationship that led to **Tymora's voluntary departure** from Ilefarn, he uncovered that the **remaining leaders became [[The Dark Six]]: The Mischief, [[The Keeper]], [[The Great Devourer|The Devourer]], The Fury, [[The Traveler]], and The Shadow.** Tracking these malevolent entities had brought Wild Fury II to [[Hartsvale]] **five years ago**, where a **supernatural, blinding rage** overwhelmed his mind and stopped him from leaving.

Now, as the party stared up a cliff-face toward the **skull of an ancient dragon** marking the entrance to their destination, a sudden **blizzard** struck, a **frost cloud avalanched** toward them, and **shadowy figures emerged**, charging through the snow in their direction.

## Key Developments

- **The father won.** **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — **Wild Fury II** — fought with **no memory of his son**, decapitated [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey the Minotaur]], knocked [[Icarus]] unconscious, struck [[Rik]] down, and axed **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]** through the chest after tearing away his shield and telling him *"You always disappointed me, son."*
- **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]'s sacrifice failed:** he took [[Rik]]'s killing blow himself to shield his father, and the dark spell did not break.
- **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] saved the party in secret** from impending death — after a **shared [[Baba Yaga]] nightmare** — and delivered them to the throne room. The Twilight Maiden is playing her own hand.
- **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain:** freedom, the voiding of **[[Vlad]]'s execution**, and **Wild Fury II's liberation** — in exchange for capturing **"[[The Devil in the Ice]]" alive** in a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]**, using an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** gifted by [[Glimmerspark Industries]]. Kholodan claims the Devil is the source of Hartsvale's nightmares.
- **Wild Fury II was manipulated** by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] into attacking his own son — he burst into the throne room furious about it.
- **Party reshuffled:** [[Gearsley]] and [[Blackjack]] had both been **captured** by Kholodan; **Gearsley** was released to join the expedition alongside **Wild Fury II**, while **[[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] stay behind as collateral**.
- **The disappearance explained:** six years ago the celebrity fairy bard **[[Dew Dew Bun]]** vanished, and Wild Fury II investigated as **head of the [[Lionshield Coster]]**. The trail brought him to [[Hartsvale]] **five years ago**, where a **supernatural blinding rage** seized his mind and trapped him there.
- **[[The Dark Six]] are finally all named:** **The Mischief, [[The Keeper]], [[The Great Devourer|The Devourer]], The Fury, [[The Traveler]], and The Shadow** — confirming [[The Traveler]] among them.
- **[[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] lore revised:** there were **seven** leaders, not six. **[[Tymora]]** departed voluntarily after a tumultuous falling-out with her sister **[[Beshaba]]**; the six who remained became the Dark Six.
- **The [[Elven Lute]]:** [[Dew Dew Bun]]'s power came from an ancient elven lute — originally **[[Tymora]]'s gift to [[Beshaba]]**.
- **Cliffhanger:** at the dragon-skull cave mouth, a blizzard, an avalanching **frost cloud**, and **shadowy figures charging through the snow**.
