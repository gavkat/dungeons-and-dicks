# Ice Golems

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
The creatures of living ice that ambushed the [[Boner Bros]] on the treacherous slopes of the [[Spine of the World]] ([[Session 17]]) — the **shadowy figures** that had come charging through the blizzard at the end of [[Session 16]]. They attacked out of an **avalanche of ice and snow**, forcing the party into a desperate uphill battle through blinding arctic winds.

Mid-skirmish, a **colossal ice titan erupted from the frost**: a far larger horror out of the same frost, and the only thing in the fight the party did not put down themselves.

## Role in Campaign
The doormen of the [[Dragon-Skull Cave|dragon-skull cave]]. Whether they guard the cave, [[The Devil in the Ice]] inside it, or nothing at all has not been established — nor has who or what made them.

The **ice titan was never killed**. [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] struck it with a **crackling lightning javelin**, and [[Icarus]] **banished it to another plane of existence** — which leaves it alive, elsewhere, and potentially returnable.
