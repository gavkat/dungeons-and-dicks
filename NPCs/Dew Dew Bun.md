# Dew Dew Bun

**Tags:** #character #npc #Mystery #Campaign2

## Description
A **celebrity fairy bard** who **vanished without a trace six years ago** ([[Session 16]]). Her fame was real enough that her disappearance registered as a matter for the [[Lionshield Coster]] — and the source of her power turned out to be far older than she was: an **[[Elven Lute|ancient elven lute]]**, originally a gift from **[[Tymora]]** to her sister **[[Beshaba]]**.

## Role in Campaign
She is the **first domino**. Her disappearance is what sent **Wild Fury II** — **[[The White Claw (Champion)|"The White Claw"]]**, then head of the [[Lionshield Coster]] — out to investigate. That investigation traced her powers to the [[Elven Lute]], the lute to the sisters [[Tymora]] and [[Beshaba]], and the sisters to the seven leaders of [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] — uncovering that the six who remained became **[[The Dark Six]]**. Tracking those entities brought Wild Fury II to [[Hartsvale]] five years ago, where a supernatural rage trapped him.

In other words: the hunt for a missing fairy bard is the reason [[Wild Fury]] grew up without a father, and the reason the party is in the frozen north at all.

Whether Dew Dew Bun is alive, who took her, and where the lute is now are all **unanswered**.
