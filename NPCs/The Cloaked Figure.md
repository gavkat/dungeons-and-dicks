# The Cloaked Figure

**Tags:** #character #npc #Mystery #Campaign2

> Not to be confused with **[[The Ashen Figure]]**, the ash-cloaked [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black" who abducted [[Dave]]. Nothing in the source connects them.

## Description
An **enigmatic cloaked figure** encountered in the **starry astral plane**, where [[Icarus]] was **briefly displaced** during the fight with the tentacled stalagmite monstrosity ([[Session 18]]). Nothing of its face, form, size, or allegiance was established.

## Role in Campaign
It said two things, and then Icarus was back:

1. A **cryptic warning about his reckless chaos**.
2. A command: ***"Fly, boy. Fly."***

Both land on a sorcerer who has now been warned about his chaos twice — [[Vraxis]] flagged **his hubris and chaotic nature** before flying away in [[Session 14]] — and whose chaotic magic keeps dragging entities across planes to meet him, the Feywild flumph [[Phlumphi]] included ([[Session 17]]). "Fly, boy" also lands on a man whose **true form is an Aarakocra** ([[Session 15]]) and whose father is [[Reginald]] — and on a man named Icarus.

Who it is, whether it is the **something in the fae realm** [[Phlumphi]] left to **report back** to, and why it could reach him on the astral plane are all open.
