# Vlad

**Tags:** #character #npc #Giant #Campaign2

## Description
A massive, **twenty-foot Frost Giant** — yet timid and hunched, nothing like the brutes his size suggests. He emerged from a screaming whiteout blizzard and stepped into the light of the party's sputtering campfire just as the gale finally died down.

Now revealed ([[Session 14]]): Vlad is **exiled from [[Hartsvale]]** — and is the **exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler**, [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich]]. His stated crime of "stealing vegetables" turned out to mean **eating disabled children**.

## Role in Campaign
Introduced at the very end of [[Session 13]], in the frozen wastes on the road north toward [[Hartsvale]]. In [[Session 14]] he became the party's guide and lore-source: sharing Hartsvale's history around the campfire — Lord [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan Ghartovich]]'s ruling style, the Twilight Maiden [[Princess Zaria]]'s rumored blessed birth, and the dream-entity [[Baba Yaga]] — and leading the [[Boner Bros]] on a **three-day trek** toward the city.

Despite the accidental cannibalism talk, the party persuaded him to **declare [[Wild Fury]] III as his champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]]** — their way into Hartsvale.

## Session History

### Session 13
- Appeared as a twenty-foot shadow over the survivors' camp, then revealed himself as a timid, hunched Frost Giant named Vlad.

### Session 14
- Shared [[Hartsvale]]'s lore around the campfire: [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich]]'s ruling style, the Twilight Maiden [[Princess Zaria]]'s rumored blessed survival at birth thanks to a wish on a shooting star, and the haunting dream-entity known as the [[Baba Yaga]].
- Guided the party on a three-day trek toward Hartsvale.
- Violently vomited when [[Wild Fury]] mercy-killed the party's mauled polar bear and fashioned its fur into a hooded jacket.
- Dropped a horrifying double-revelation: he is the **exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler**, and his "stealing vegetables" actually meant **eating disabled children**.
- Was persuaded to declare [[Wild Fury]] III his champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]] so the party could enter Hartsvale.

### Session 15
- Entered [[Hartsvale]] with [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] and led them to the Krug training camp.
- Explained the **legal loophole** driving the plan: if [[Wild Fury]] fights and defeats his father as Vlad's **sworn champion** in the Krug, victory **voids Vlad's exile** — without forcing Wild Fury to remain trapped under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Ghartovich]] as the replacement "Maiden's Champion."
