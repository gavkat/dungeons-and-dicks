# Yeti-Tykes

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**Juvenile yetis** denning inside the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] — small enough to be called tykes, bold enough to rob an armed party in its sleep. They are the answer to the **unseen thief** that got past [[Gearsley]]'s alert watch in [[Session 17]] and left with the party's **gold**, **all their rations**, **one of Gearsley's guns**, and [[Rik]]'s **[[Flame Tongue Whip]]**.

The **nine-foot yeti petrified in stone** at that same campsite ([[Session 17]]) was, per [[Archibald]]'s letter, turned to stone by the goblin with a **stolen wand** — long before the party arrived.

## Role in Campaign
Comic relief with a body count on their own side. In [[Session 18]] the party met them twice:

- **In the petrified forest** — [[Rik]] caught one thieving, **smacked it with an offered fish**, and as it fled [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **test-fired his new [[Wand of Wonder]]** and **splattered it against the cavern wall** with a gale.
- **In [[Vraxis]]'s archive** — the survivors were found **playing with the party's stolen gear**. A goose-shaped [[Icarus]] **flared his wings and honked aggressively**, panicking the tyke holding **[[Gearsley]]'s firearm** into **shooting itself** — which is how [[Gearsley]] got his **[[Thoughts & Prayers|twin pistols]]** back.

## Session History

### Session 17
- *(In hindsight.)* Raided the party's camp during their extended rest, stealing the gold, the rations, one of [[Gearsley]]'s guns, and the [[Flame Tongue Whip]] without anyone seeing them.

### Session 18
- One was **caught thieving by [[Rik]]** in the petrified forest and **smacked with an offered fish**, then **killed** by the gale from [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]'s newly attuned [[Wand of Wonder]] as it fled.
- The **survivors** were found in **[[Vraxis]]'s subterranean archive**, playing with the party's stolen gear.
- One **shot itself** with [[Gearsley]]'s stolen firearm after a honking, wing-flaring goose-[[Icarus]] panicked it — **returning the gun to its owner**.
