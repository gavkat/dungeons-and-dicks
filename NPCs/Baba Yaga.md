# Baba Yaga

**Tags:** #character #npc #Antagonist #Mystery #Campaign2

## Description
A haunting **dream-entity** of the frozen north, spoken of in [[Hartsvale]]'s lore by [[Vlad]] ([[Session 14]]). It invades sleepers' nightmares — psychic warfare that deals **waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia**. Its true nature is unknown.

## Role in Campaign
The arctic nights of the party's three-day trek toward [[Hartsvale]] devolved into psychic warfare as the Baba Yaga invaded everyone's nightmares. Its madness has a body count: the gruesome **border outpost** the party bypassed on the way into Hartsvale was littered with the **self-mutilated victims** of the entity's influence.
