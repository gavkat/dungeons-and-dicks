# Reginald

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**[[Icarus]]'s father.** Long known only as the source of the restricted [[Sending Phone]] and Icarus's family expectations ([[Session 1]]), he was named in [[Session 15]]. As the father of Icarus — whose true form is an **Aarakocra** — he is presumably an Aarakocra himself.

## Role in Campaign
Not yet met on-screen, and it is unclear whether he is even in [[Hartsvale]]. In [[Session 15]], [[Icarus]] spotted **Kenku guards** in the city and **assumed** they belonged to Reginald. His childhood friend [[Kenky]] — one of those Kenku — exploited that assumption, **mimicking Reginald's voice** to lure Icarus into a trap. What (if anything) actually ties Reginald to Hartsvale remains unknown.
