# The White Claw (Champion)

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

> Not to be confused with **[[The White Claw]]**, the arena (the Krug) that bears his name.

## Description
**Wild Fury II** — the **Maiden's Champion** of [[Hartsvale]]'s [[The White Claw|Krug]], and **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]'s long-lost father**. A ferocious **Tabaxi** who fights in a **white-hot rage** wielding a **great axe**; the arena art shows him tiger-headed and mane-braided, wreathed in dark red fury. Introduced by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] as **"The White Claw"** ([[Session 15]]), named as **Wild Fury II** in [[Session 16]].

Before Hartsvale, he was **head of the [[Lionshield Coster]]** — the covert guild his son later inherited in his absence.

## The Rage
The rage is **not his**, and it is **not over**. When the fairy bard [[Dew Dew Bun]]'s trail brought him to [[Hartsvale]] **five years ago**, a **supernatural, blinding rage overwhelmed his mind and stopped him from leaving** ([[Session 16]]). Under it he fought his own son **with no memory of him** — and [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] **manipulated him into that fight**, a betrayal Wild Fury II confronted the lord over the moment he was lucid again. His son's attempt to break the spell by taking a killing blow for him **failed**.

It **took him again** in [[Session 18]], deep beneath the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]: he groaned under a **surge of supernatural bloodlust**, went at [[Vraxis]] frenzied, and **hacked through the chamber's load-bearing pillars**. What finally broke it was the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** — the device [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] sent the party to cage a monster with — **snapped onto him by his own son**.

## Role in Campaign
The mysterious **[[The White Claw|Krug]] flyer** that reached [[Wild Fury]] at [[Holly Hollow]] ([[Session 12]]) had pointed toward his missing father all along — the thread that drove the party's trek to [[Hartsvale]]. In [[Session 15]] the intrigued king summoned him into the arena and Wild Fury recognized him. In [[Session 16]] he **won that fight**, cutting down [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey]], [[Icarus]], [[Rik]], and finally his son.

**Liberated** as part of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain, he travelled **with** the party into the [[Spine of the World]] — and it was on that trek that he finally explained the six missing years: [[Dew Dew Bun]], the [[Elven Lute]], [[Tymora]] and [[Beshaba]] among the **seven [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] leaders**, and the naming of all of **[[The Dark Six]]**.

## The Last Stand
In the cave-in that ended [[Session 18]], a **massive granite monolith sheared loose** above [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]. In a **blur of white fur** his father **tackled him into the snow** and **caught the crushing mountain across his own back**. Bracing the **collapsing stone jaws with his bare paws**, he **ordered his son to run**, declaring that **after fighting for other men, he would die on his own terms** — the first thing he ever gave his son freely, and an epitaph he wrote himself.

[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] lunged with the **[[Immovable Rod]]** and his **footing gave way**; the rod locked into empty air **inches short of the stone**. **Millions of tons of rock crashed down, sealing him within the dark.** The source stops there. He was not seen to die.

## Session History

### Session 15
- Summoned into the arena as the Maiden's Champion, introduced as **"The White Claw."**
- Stepped forth wielding a great axe in a white-hot rage; [[Wild Fury]] recognized him as **his own father**.

### Session 16
- Fought **shrouded in bloodlust with no memory of his son**: **decapitated [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey the Minotaur]]**, knocked [[Icarus]] unconscious with a brutal retaliatory blow, and was left battered and staggering on the edge of death by [[Rik]]'s counteroffensive.
- When **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] took [[Rik]]'s killing blow for him**, the sacrifice **failed to break the dark spell**. He struck [[Rik]] down, then **ripped away his son's shield**, said ***"You always disappointed me, son,"*** and brought his great axe down in a strike that **concaved Wild Fury III's chest**.
- **Burst into the throne room** during the party's audience, **furious at [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] for manipulating him into attacking his own son**.
- **Liberated** as one of the three terms of Kholodan's bargain, and joined the party's expedition into the [[Spine of the World]] alongside [[Gearsley]].
- On the three-day trek, **revealed the truth behind his disappearance**: the celebrity fairy bard [[Dew Dew Bun]] vanished **six years ago**; investigating as **head of the [[Lionshield Coster]]**, he traced her powers to an **[[Elven Lute|ancient elven lute]]** — [[Tymora]]'s gift to her sister [[Beshaba]], **two of the seven [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]] leaders**. Tymora departed voluntarily; the remaining six became **[[The Dark Six]]: The Mischief, [[The Keeper]], [[The Great Devourer|The Devourer]], The Fury, [[The Traveler]], and The Shadow**. Tracking them brought him to [[Hartsvale]] **five years ago**, where the rage took him.

### Session 17
- Fought the [[Ice Golems|ice golems]] on the mountain slopes, **effortlessly outpacing his son** through the melee while [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] repeatedly sought his validation — the **awkward tension** between them simmering through the whole battle.
- **Dismissed his son's attempts to establish team hierarchy** inside the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] and **stubbornly took the lead down the left corridor**, straight into the **gold illusion**. Ensnared alongside [[Gearsley]] and [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]], he **bluntly snapped at his son's warnings** and waded into the **acidic sludge**; the party pulled all three out.
- **Hacked the head clean off** the **nine-foot petrified yeti** looming over the old campsite in the right corridor, securing the perimeter for the party's extended rest. *(That yeti had been petrified by the goblin [[Archibald]], [[Session 18]].)*

### Session 18
- **Shared a tender moment with his son** in the petrified forest, recognizing the crooked **[[Wand of Wonder]]** [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] had just attuned to as a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard** — the warmest exchange the two have had.
- **The bloodlust returned.** He **groaned under a surge of supernatural bloodlust** in [[Vraxis]]'s archive, attacked the dragon **frenzied**, and **inadvertently hacked through the chamber's load-bearing pillars**, fracturing the cavern.
- **Collared by his own son.** [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] used the chaos of [[Vraxis]]'s attack on [[Icarus]] to **snap the [[Arcane Dampener Collar]] onto him**, **breaking the bloodlust**.
- **Caught the mountain.** As the cavern collapsed, he **tackled his son clear** of a falling **granite monolith** and took it across his **own back**, bracing the **stone jaws bare-pawed** at the dragon-skull threshold.
- **Ordered his son to run**, saying that after a lifetime **fighting for other men, he would die on his own terms** — and was **sealed within the dark** when the [[Immovable Rod]] locked short and millions of tons of rock came down.
