# The Traveler

**Tags:** #character #npc #Mystery #DarkSix

## Description
A shrouded figure who deals in cursed gifts.

## Role in Campaign
Made the dark pact that gave [[Riptide]] the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]] — the amulet now bound to [[Blackjack]]. Identity and motives remain unknown, but the **allegiance is settled**: [[Session 16]] **confirmed the Traveler as one of [[The Dark Six]]**, named by **Wild Fury II** alongside The Mischief ([[Beshaba]]), [[The Keeper]], [[The Great Devourer|The Devourer]], The Fury, and The Shadow.

Which means the cursed amulet handed to [[Riptide]] was **a god's gift** — one Dark Six member putting another's [[Shard of the Great Devourer|Shard]] into mortal hands.

## Session History

### Session 3
- Named in [[Riptide]]'s confession as the source of the cursed amulet.

### Session 16
- **Confirmed as a member of [[The Dark Six]]** when **Wild Fury II** named the full roster on the trek into the [[Spine of the World]].
