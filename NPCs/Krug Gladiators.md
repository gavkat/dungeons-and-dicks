# The Krug Gladiators

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
The band of fellow fighters [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] met inside the prison-like training camp of [[Hartsvale]]'s [[The White Claw|Krug]] arena ([[Session 15]]):

- **Shank** — a Tiefling with glass horns. *(Killed in the second wave of the trials.)*
- **Mother** — a Bullywug. *(Crushed by the anti-magic snail's flail in the third wave.)*
- **Icey** — a Goblin. *(Killed in the second wave of the trials.)*
- **Humphrey** — a Minotaur. *(Decapitated by the Maiden's Champion, [[Session 16]].)*

## Role in Campaign
After a two-day training montage, the gladiators were marched into the arena for the **Krug combat trials** before [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]. They dispatched a first wave of tigers, but a **crag cat and a four-armed gorillon** killed **Icey** and **Shank** in the second wave, and a massive **anti-magic snail** crushed **Mother** in the third — before an unrecognized [[Icarus]] banished it.
