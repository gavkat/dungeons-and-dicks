# Gnoll Enforcers

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**Three elite gnoll enforcers** guarding [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s throne room in [[Hartsvale]], wielding **three-headed flails** ([[Session 19]]). The session art shows them hunched and snarling before Kholodan's glowing orb — one dragging a flail of **red-eyed skulls**. Kholodan has kept **gnoll honor guards** at his flanks since the party first saw the throne room ([[Session 16]]).

## Role in Campaign
The last line between the uprising and the Lord. When the party breached the throne room, **paralyzing toxins and psychic strikes pinned the frontline** (the source does not say whose) — until [[Icarus]]'s wild surge turned **the whole party invisible** and the fight became an ambush. As **his guard crumbled**, Kholodan pulled the lever that released [[Vlad]]. The last of them died in [[Session 20]], **impaled on [[Dragon Saver]]**.

## Session History

### Session 19
- Defended [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s protective orb in the throne room with **three-headed flails**, as **paralyzing toxins and psychic strikes** pinned the party's frontline.
- **Crumbled** under the invisible party's ambush, driving Kholodan to drop [[Vlad]]'s cage.

### Session 20
- **The remaining gnoll honor guard** was **impaled by [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] with [[Dragon Saver]]**.
