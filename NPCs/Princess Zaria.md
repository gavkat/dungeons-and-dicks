# Princess Zaria

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**The Twilight Maiden** of [[Hartsvale]], rumored to owe her **blessed survival at birth to a wish on a shooting star** ([[Vlad]]'s campfire lore, [[Session 14]]). Depicted pale, fair-haired and **pointed-eared** beneath a gold crown, in a maroon-and-gold gown.

## Role in Campaign
First seen in [[Session 15]], welcoming guests at the castle and watching the **Krug combat trials** beside [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]. She is the **"maiden"** whose champion — revealed to be **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**, [[Wild Fury]]'s father — the [[The White Claw]] flyer dared Wild Fury to face ([[Session 12]]).

**She saved the party's lives ([[Session 16]]) — and did it in secret.** After the Maiden's Champion left the [[Boner Bros]] dying on the arena floor, Zarya **secretly** pulled them back from impending death in an infirmary, then escorted them to the throne room herself. The Twilight Maiden saved the very fighters **her own champion** had just cut down — and did it in secret, in a court where [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] had manipulated that champion into the fight. Her motives are entirely unstated.

## Session History

### Session 14
- Named in [[Vlad]]'s telling of [[Hartsvale]]'s lore as the Twilight Maiden, rumored blessed at birth by a wish on a shooting star.

### Session 15
- Seen (as "Princess Zarya") welcoming guests at [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s opulent castle and presiding over the **Krug combat trials**, where her champion **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** was summoned to face [[Wild Fury]].

### Session 16
- **Secretly saved the party from impending death** after her champion cut them down in the arena; they awoke in an **infirmary** to find out she was responsible.
- **Escorted them to the grand throne room** for their audience with [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]].
