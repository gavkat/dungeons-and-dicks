# Princess Zaria

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

> **Revealed ([[Session 20]]):** Zarya was the **[[Baba Yaga]]** — a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**, born of her father's bargain with a falling star and [[The Dark Six]]. Her crown's **obsidian star** was its phylactery, and the party destroyed it.

## Description
**The Twilight Maiden** of [[Hartsvale]], daughter of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] (implied in [[Session 20]], when he died begging for his daughter's life), rumored to owe her **blessed survival at birth to a wish on a shooting star** ([[Vlad]]'s campfire lore, [[Session 14]]). Depicted pale, fair-haired and **pointed-eared** beneath a gold crown, in a maroon-and-gold gown.

## Role in Campaign
First seen in [[Session 15]], welcoming guests at the castle and watching the **Krug combat trials** beside [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]. She is the **"maiden"** whose champion — revealed to be **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**, [[Wild Fury]]'s father — the [[The White Claw]] flyer dared Wild Fury to face ([[Session 12]]).

**She saved the party's lives ([[Session 16]]) — and did it in secret.** After the Maiden's Champion left the [[Boner Bros]] dying on the arena floor, Zarya **secretly** pulled them back from impending death in an infirmary, then escorted them to the throne room herself. The Twilight Maiden saved the very fighters **her own champion** had just cut down — and did it in secret, in a court where [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] had manipulated that champion into the fight. Her motives are entirely unstated.

**What she was ([[Session 20]]).** After [[Rik]] killed her father — who died **pleading for her life** — the mob turned on her with the [[The White Fury|white fury]] in their eyes, and the party smuggled her out **invisible** on [[Icarus]]'s pterodactyl back. In a tavern suite she grieved for her people, saying a true ruler should be **a hearth for their kingdom rather than freezing the valley to keep a single candle lit**, and showed them the **obsidian star** at the core of her crown — **the relic of a wish made upon a falling star when her mother died** — whose **starlit twilight** lulled them to rest. Then, glassy-eyed, she **unfolded into the [[Baba Yaga]]**: jaw past her collarbone, spine inverted, skin split into bone needles. The nightmares that followed showed **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] bargaining with a falling star and [[The Dark Six]] to birth an avatar of fury** as his wife died. The party read it as a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**, the star as its **phylactery**, and **shattered it**. Whether anything of the girl who saved them in [[Session 16]] was real — or survives — the source does not say.

## Session History

### Session 14
- Named in [[Vlad]]'s telling of [[Hartsvale]]'s lore as the Twilight Maiden, rumored blessed at birth by a wish on a shooting star.

### Session 15
- Seen (as "Princess Zarya") welcoming guests at [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s opulent castle and presiding over the **Krug combat trials**, where her champion **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** was summoned to face [[Wild Fury]].

### Session 16
- **Secretly saved the party from impending death** after her champion cut them down in the arena; they awoke in an **infirmary** to find out she was responsible.
- **Escorted them to the grand throne room** for their audience with [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]].

### Session 20
- Her father **pleaded for her life** before [[Rik]] beheaded him; the **white-fury mob** then turned on her, and the party **flew her out invisible** on [[Icarus]]'s pterodactyl form.
- In the vacant king's suite of a tavern, grieved for her people — **a ruler should be a hearth, not freeze the valley to keep a single candle lit** — and revealed the **obsidian star** in her crown, a **wish made upon a falling star when her mother died**, using it to cast a **soothing, starlit twilight** for the party's rest.
- **Became the [[Baba Yaga]]** mid-rest: **jaw dislocated past her collarbone**, **spine inverted**, **skin split into bone needles**, gown shed to reveal the **iron-toothed** crone.
- Identified as a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**; her husk was **melted to slag** by [[Icarus]] and her crown's star — the **phylactery** — **shattered** with the [[Immovable Rod]].
