# Princess Zaria

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**The Twilight Maiden** of [[Hartsvale]], known so far only through [[Vlad]]'s campfire lore ([[Session 14]]): she is rumored to owe her **blessed survival at birth to a wish on a shooting star**.

## Role in Campaign
Not yet met — but she is the **"maiden"** whose champion the [[The White Claw]] flyer dared [[Wild Fury]] to face ([[Session 12]]). With Wild Fury now declared [[Vlad]]'s champion at the Krug ([[Session 14]]), a clash with her champion looms.

## Session History

### Session 14
