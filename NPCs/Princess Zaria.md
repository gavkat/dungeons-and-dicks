# Princess Zaria

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**The Twilight Maiden** of [[Hartsvale]], known so far only through [[Vlad]]'s campfire lore ([[Session 14]]): she is rumored to owe her **blessed survival at birth to a wish on a shooting star**.
