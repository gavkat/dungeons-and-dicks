# The Devil in the Ice

**Tags:** #character #npc #Antagonist #Mystery #Campaign2

## Description
Whatever is waiting at the bottom of a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]**. [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] names it **"the Devil in the Ice"** and **claims** it to be the **source of the nightmares plaguing [[Hartsvale]]** ([[Session 16]]) — a claim the party has no way to verify, and which sits uneasily beside the [[Baba Yaga]], the dream-entity already blamed for exactly that.

Nothing about its nature, size, or species has been established. What *is* established is that Kholodan wants it **alive**, and expects it to need an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar|arcane dampener collar]]** to stay that way.

## Role in Campaign
The price of the party's freedom. Under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]), capturing the Devil in the Ice alive buys the [[Boner Bros]] their liberty, voids **[[Vlad]]'s execution**, and frees **Wild Fury II** — with [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] held as collateral until it's done.

That a frost giant lord would rather have this thing **caged than killed** is the loudest thing about it.
