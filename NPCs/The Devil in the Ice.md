# The Devil in the Ice

**Tags:** #character #npc #Antagonist #Mystery #Campaign2

## Description
Whatever is waiting at the bottom of a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]**. [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] names it **"the Devil in the Ice"** and **claims** it to be the **source of the nightmares plaguing [[Hartsvale]]** ([[Session 16]]) — a claim the party has no way to verify, and which sits uneasily beside the [[Baba Yaga]], the dream-entity already blamed for exactly that.

Nothing about its nature, size, or species has been established. What *is* established is that Kholodan wants it **alive**, and expects it to need an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar|arcane dampener collar]]** to stay that way.

[[Session 18]] added two things and took one away. It added a **second hunter**: the goblin adventurer **[[Archibald]]**, who came into the mountain after the Devil to **cure his brothers' afflictions** and froze to death for it — so the Devil is known outside [[Hartsvale]], and at least one person believed it could **cure** something. And it added a **denial**: the party decided the Devil was **[[Vraxis]]**, collared him for it, and were told flatly that the mountain **merely echoes nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle**.

What it took away is the cave. The party left the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] **collapsed behind them**, without ever meeting the thing they were sent for.

## Role in Campaign
The price of the party's freedom. Under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]), capturing the Devil in the Ice alive buys the [[Boner Bros]] their liberty, voids **[[Vlad]]'s execution**, and frees **Wild Fury II** — with [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] held as collateral until it's done.

That a frost giant lord would rather have this thing **caged than killed** is the loudest thing about it.

## Session History

### Session 16
- Named by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] in the throne room as the target of the party's bargain: enter a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]** and take it **alive** with the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**.
- Claimed by Kholodan to be the source of [[Hartsvale]]'s nightmares.
- The party reached the cliff-face beneath the **skull of an ancient dragon** — and were met instead by a blizzard, an avalanching frost cloud, and **shadowy figures charging through the snow**.

### Session 17
- The party **entered its supposed lair** — the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] — and worked through both corridors past a **gold illusion**, an **acid pool**, and a **petrified yeti** without meeting it. Still nothing established about what it is.
- Nothing in the cave has yet been tied to the Devil. Whether the **[[Ice Golems|ice golems]]** on the slopes, the illusion, or the **unseen thief** that robbed the party in their sleep answer to it is unknown.

### Session 18
- **A previous hunter surfaced.** The goblin **[[Archibald]]**, found frozen below the mural, had come for the Devil in the Ice to **cure his brothers' afflictions** — and had **petrified the campsite's yeti** with a stolen wand along the way.
- **The party guessed wrong.** Convinced the Devil was **[[Vraxis]]**, "aka [[Baba Yaga]]," they ambushed the cobalt dragon with the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**. He **scoffed at the insult** and said the mountain **merely echoes nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle**.
- **The errand is in ruins.** The collar was spent on **Wild Fury II**, the cave **collapsed**, and the Devil — if it is down there at all — was **never seen**.
- The **unseen thief** was resolved as [[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]], not the Devil or any servant of it.
