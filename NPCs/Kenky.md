# Kenky

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
A **Kenku** and a **childhood friend of [[Icarus]]**. He is found among the Kenku guards in [[Hartsvale]] whom Icarus assumed belonged to his father, **[[Reginald]]**.

## Role in Campaign
When [[Icarus]] made telepathic contact with one of the Kenku guards ([[Session 15]]), that Kenku turned out to be Kenky. Though Icarus wore his half-orc body rather than his **Aarakocra** form, Kenky admitted that he **knew who Icarus really was**, and arranged to meet him at the west wall that night. There, using a Kenku's gift for **mimicry**, Kenky imitated the voice of Icarus's father to lure him in — then **trapped Icarus inside a sealed metal box**, which was later delivered into the Krug's training ground.
