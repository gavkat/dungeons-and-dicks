# Lord Kholodan Ghartovich

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
The **ruler of [[Hartsvale]]**, known so far only through the campfire lore of [[Vlad]] ([[Session 14]]) — who described his ruling style, and later confessed to being the **exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler**.

Seen properly in [[Session 16]]: **blue-skinned, white-haired and pointed-eared**, crowned in gold and seated on a **rune-carved ice throne** with a **frost scepter**, before a hearth bearing a **stag-and-gear emblem**, attended by **gnoll honor guards**.

## Role in Campaign
First seen in [[Session 15]], presiding over [[Hartsvale]]: welcoming guests at his opulent castle and overseeing the **Krug combat trials** from the stands beside the Twilight Maiden, [[Princess Zaria]]. He is the ruler whose exile [[Wild Fury]] can lift — or bind himself to — through the Krug, per [[Vlad]]'s loophole.

**[[Session 16]] recast him as the campaign's manipulator, not its host.** Wild Fury II burst into the throne room **furious at him for manipulating him into attacking his own son**. He had also **quietly captured [[Gearsley]] and [[Blackjack]]**. And with the party at his mercy he traded all of it away — their freedom, [[Vlad]]'s execution, Wild Fury II's liberation — for one errand: **[[The Devil in the Ice]]**, taken **alive**, out of a dragon-skull cave in the [[Spine of the World]]. He kept [[Blackjack]] and his own brother [[Vlad]] as collateral.

Why a frost giant lord wants that thing **caged rather than dead** — and whether his story about it causing [[Hartsvale]]'s nightmares is true — is the open question.

## Session History

### Session 14
- Named in [[Vlad]]'s telling of [[Hartsvale]]'s lore, which detailed his ruling style.
- [[Vlad]] later revealed himself to be the exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler.

### Session 15
- Seen welcoming guests at his opulent castle as a mammoth-led royal procession arrived, alongside [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]].
- Presided over the **Krug combat trials**. Intrigued by [[Wild Fury]]'s theatrical showmanship after [[Icarus]] banished the anti-magic snail, he officially summoned the Maiden's Champion, **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — [[Wild Fury]]'s father — into the arena.

### Session 16
- Held court in the **grand throne room**, flanked by **gnoll honor guards** and the **muzzled, chained** [[Vlad]], when [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] escorted the revived party in.
- **Accused to his face** by Wild Fury II — who burst through the doors — of **manipulating him into attacking his own son**.
- Revealed he had **captured [[Gearsley]] and [[Blackjack]]**.
- **Offered his bargain:** the party's **freedom**, the **voiding of [[Vlad]]'s execution**, and **Wild Fury II's liberation**, in exchange for entering a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]** and capturing **"[[The Devil in the Ice]]" alive** with an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** gifted by [[Glimmerspark Industries]] — claiming the Devil to be the source of [[Hartsvale]]'s nightmares.
- **Persuaded to release [[Gearsley]]** to the expedition alongside Wild Fury II, while **keeping [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] as collateral**.
