# Vraxis

**Tags:** #character #npc #Dragon #Campaign2

## Description
A terrifying, **eight-foot-tall cobalt dragon soldier**, first encountered disguised as an illusionary bird high above the frozen wastes. A lethal, disciplined warrior — and, to his own exasperation, the object of [[Icarus]]'s delusional romantic pursuit.

[[Session 18]] adds a second face to the soldier: a **cobalt draconic scholar** who keeps a **lavish subterranean archive** deep beneath the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] — and **cooks**, well enough that the party tracked him down by following **sizzling smoked sausages and Cajun spices**. The session art has him aproned over a cauldron, the apron reading *"Kiss the Chef."*

## Role in Campaign
Revealed when a polymorphed [[Icarus]] dive-bombed his bird illusion ([[Session 14]]). He could have killed the sorcerer outright, but was deeply intrigued by [[Polly]] — sensing the parrot was **no mere bird, but a mysterious fey entity**. When Icarus doubled down on the fantasy that Vraxis was courting him (even inviting the dragon on a date where Vraxis would pay), Vraxis routed him with a potent fear spell, endured an excruciatingly awkward walk in the same direction, then **warned Icarus of his hubris and chaotic nature before flying away for good**.

"For good" lasted four sessions. In [[Session 18]] the party walked into his archive and **mistook him for "[[The Devil in the Ice|the devil in the ice]]," aka the [[Baba Yaga]]**, and tried to ambush him with the **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**. He **scoffed at the insult** and gave them the single most consequential line of the session: the mountain **merely echoes nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle**. Then he unleashed a **tempest of frost and lightning**, and when [[Icarus]] tried to bargain by **claiming to be an immortal god**, Vraxis — **amused** — **rocketed him through the ceiling, stopped his heart with lightning, and threw his body into the abyss**.

He warned Icarus about his hubris in [[Session 14]]. In [[Session 18]] the sorcerer staked a bargain on being an **immortal god** — and Vraxis, amused, obliged him with a lethal jolt of lightning.

Who — or what — Vraxis soldiers for is still unknown, as is **what he was doing with an archive under a dragon-skull cave**.

**[[Session 19]] turned him from executioner to patron.** He **survived the cave-in** and came to the party bearing **parting gifts and visions**: a memory of post-attack [[Leilon]] for [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] and the reforging of the Dragon Slayer into **[[Dragon Saver]]**; a halt to [[Rik]]'s **parasitic scorpion armor** and a **scrying vision** of [[Marisya]] and [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]; and the **awakening of [[Polly]]** as a **Chwinga**. Then he **conjured a blizzard** and **teleported the party into [[Hartsvale]]** for their uprising against [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]. His one complaint was [[Icarus]] drinking his **private reserve** of **"Leomund's Tiny Nut"**.

## Session History

### Session 14
- Unmasked mid-air by [[Icarus]]'s dive-bomb; knocked the sorcerer back into half-orc form but spared him out of intrigue at [[Polly]].
- Descended a second time; fed up with Icarus's delusional courtship, bypassed his defenses with a fear spell and sent him sprinting away in terror.
- After the awkward walk that followed, warned Icarus of his hubris and chaotic nature, then flew away for good.

### Session 18
- Found keeping a **lavish subterranean archive** beneath the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]], named as a **cobalt draconic scholar**; the party tracked him by the smell of **smoked sausages and Cajun spices**.
- **Ambushed by the party with the [[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**, who were convinced he was **"the devil in the ice," aka the [[Baba Yaga]]**. He **scoffed at the insult**.
- **Declared the mountain merely echoed nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle** — contradicting Kholodan's [[Session 16]] claim outright.
- Unleashed a **tempest of frost and lightning**; a **frenzied [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** attacked him and hacked through the chamber's **load-bearing pillars**, fracturing the cavern.
- **Killed [[Icarus]]**: amused by the sorcerer's bargain that he was an **immortal god**, Vraxis **rocketed him through the ceiling**, **stopped his heart with a lethal jolt of lightning**, and **hurled his limp body into the abyss**.

### Session 19
- **Survived the cave-in** and arrived with **parting gifts and visions** for the party.
- Guided [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] into a **memory of dragon-damaged [[Leilon]] five years earlier** and **reshaped the Dragon Slayer sword into [[Dragon Saver]]**.
- **Halted the spread of [[Rik]]'s parasitic scorpion armor**, condensing it into a **scorpion-plated crest** with a **five-headed dragon sigil**, and granted Rik a **scrying vision** of [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]] and the tethered [[Marisya]].
- **Awakened [[Polly]]**, revealing her to be a **Chwinga** fey spirit.
- **Scolded [[Icarus]]** for drinking his **private reserve** — a potion labelled **"Leomund's Tiny Nut."**
- **Conjured a raging blizzard** as cover and **teleported the party into [[Hartsvale]]'s slums** for the uprising against [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]].
