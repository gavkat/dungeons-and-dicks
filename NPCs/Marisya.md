# Marisya

**Tags:** #character #npc #QuestTarget

## Description
[[Rik]]'s wife — ailing under a curse — and mother of his son **[[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]** ([[Session 19]]). In [[Vraxis]]'s scrying vision she lay **bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering** — wording that, for the first time, links her affliction to **[[The Tethered]]**.

## Role in Campaign
The reason Rik adventures. The cure lead from [[Riptide]] pointed to [[Anauroch]] and the [[Tomb of Tzentak]]; with [[Tzentak]] destroyed, whether a cure was actually found there remains an open question. **Time is short ([[Session 19]]):** a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** in [[Hartsvale]] called her Rik's **dying wife**.

## Session History

### Session 1
- Her illness and the [[Anauroch]] cure lead introduced.

### Session 5

### Session 19
- Seen in [[Vraxis]]'s **scrying vision** for [[Rik]]: **bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering**, beside their son [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]] — while **a stranger comforted the boy outside**.
- Called Rik's **dying wife** by a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** who warned him he was wasting precious time away from her.
