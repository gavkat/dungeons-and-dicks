## Overview
The frozen range the party was sent into under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]) — **three days' trek across the arctic tundra** from [[Hartsvale]].

Their destination is a **cave whose entrance is marked by the skull of an ancient dragon**, set into a **cliff-face**, and their quarry is **[[The Devil in the Ice]]**, to be taken alive with an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**.

## Session History

### Session 16
- The party crossed the arctic tundra toward the range over **three days**, joined by **Wild Fury II** and [[Gearsley]] — the trek during which Wild Fury II finally explained his disappearance, the [[Elven Lute]], and the naming of [[The Dark Six]].
- Arriving beneath the **dragon-skull cave** entrance, they were struck by a **sudden blizzard**, an **avalanching frost cloud**, and **shadowy figures charging through the snow** toward them.
