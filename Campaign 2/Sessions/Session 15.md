# Session 15: The Krug

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** The [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark]] convoy reaches [[Hartsvale]]; [[Blackjack]] and [[Gearsley]] are turned away at the gates; [[Icarus]] is lured and trapped by his childhood friend [[Kenky]]; [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fight the Krug's combat trials alongside doomed gladiators; and the Maiden's Champion — **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"** — is revealed to be Wild Fury's own father.

## Summary

The [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark]] convoy reached the industrial, soot-belching city of [[Hartsvale]]. Disguised with a soot mustache, a Glimmerspark Industries working ID pass, and a jacket, [[Icarus]] slipped past the loading-dock guards to infiltrate the city walls. Elsewhere, [[Vlad]] explained a **legal loophole** to the rest of the group: if [[Wild Fury]] fights and defeats his father as Vlad's sworn champion in the Krug arena, victory will **void Vlad's exile** — without forcing Wild Fury to stay trapped under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Ghartovich]] as the replacement "Maiden's Champion." At the main gates, the Orc guards refused entry to [[Blackjack]] and [[Gearsley]], stating that **human and warforged were not welcome** in the city due to ancient resentment over [[Phandelver's Pact]]. Gearsley and Blackjack remained at an eastern outpost while Vlad, [[Rik]], and Wild Fury entered Hartsvale and headed straight to the training camp to prepare for the Krug.

Inside the city, [[Icarus]] navigated the filthy underbelly of [[Hartsvale]], eventually coming across a mammoth-led royal procession leading to an opulent castle where [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] welcomed guests. Icarus spotted **Kenku guards** and, assuming they belonged to his father, **[[Reginald]]**, made telepathic contact with one of them. Despite being in his half-orc form and not in his **Aarakocra body**, Icarus persuaded the Kenku that he was a friend of Reginald's son. The Kenku told Icarus to meet him later that night at the west wall. When they met, the Kenku revealed himself to be a childhood friend of Icarus named **[[Kenky]]**, admitting that he knew who Icarus really was. Lured by the voice of his father, Icarus was tricked by Kenky's mimicry and **trapped inside a sealed metal box**.

Inside the prison-like training camp, [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] met a group of fellow [[Krug Gladiators|gladiators]]: **Shank** the Tiefling with glass horns, **Mother** the Bullywug, **Icey** the Goblin, and **Humphrey** the Minotaur. During this time, the trapped [[Icarus]] was delivered to the training ground — but the rest of the party did not realize it was Icarus inside the steel box. Following a two-day training montage, the fighters were brought to the main arena for the **Krug combat trials**. Greeted by a massive cheering crowd, they stood before [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]. In the first wave, the gladiators quickly dispatched a group of attacking tigers. The second wave unleashed a **crag cat and a four-armed gorillon**, which killed both **Icey** and **Shank** before being taken down. A massive **anti-magic snail** arrived in the third wave, squashing **Mother** under its flail — until Icarus, having turned into a **potted plant** thanks to a wild-magic surge, successfully cast **banishment**.

With the snail gone, [[Wild Fury]] performed a theatrical, intimidating presentation to address [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and hype up the crowd, buying enough time to **permanently remove the elemental snail** from the battlefield. Intrigued by the spectacle, the king officially summoned the Maiden's Champion into the arena, introducing him as **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**. As the ferocious gladiator stepped forth wielding a great axe in a white-hot rage, Wild Fury turned around and **recognized the champion as his own father**.

## Key Developments

- **Party split at the gates:** [[Blackjack]] (human) and [[Gearsley]] — revealed to be a **warforged** — were refused entry to [[Hartsvale]] over **ancient resentment tied to [[Phandelver's Pact]]**, and stayed behind at an eastern outpost. Only [[Vlad]], [[Rik]], and [[Wild Fury]] entered the city.
- **[[Vlad]]'s loophole:** if [[Wild Fury]] fights and defeats his father as Vlad's **sworn champion** at the Krug, victory voids Vlad's exile — without trapping Wild Fury as [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Ghartovich]]'s replacement "Maiden's Champion."
- **[[Icarus]] captured:** lured by **[[Kenky]]** — a Kenku childhood friend among the guards Icarus took for his father **[[Reginald]]**'s — who mimicked his father's voice and sealed him inside a metal box, then delivered him unknowingly into the arena.
- **New lore on [[Icarus]]:** his true form is an **Aarakocra**; his father is named as **[[Reginald]]**, and Icarus assumed the city's Kenku served him.
- **The Krug trials:** [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fought three waves alongside the [[Krug Gladiators]]. Gladiators **Icey, Shank, and Mother died**; a still-unrecognized [[Icarus]] (as a **potted plant**) banished the anti-magic elemental snail, and [[Wild Fury]]'s theatrical stalling made the banishment permanent.
- **The reveal:** the Maiden's Champion, **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**, is [[Wild Fury]]'s long-lost father — the very quarry the [[The White Claw|Krug]] flyer had been pointing toward all along.
