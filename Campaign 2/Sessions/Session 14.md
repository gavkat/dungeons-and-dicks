# Session 14: Exiled and Exhausted

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** [[Icarus]] survives the dragon soldier [[Vraxis]] — spared only by [[Polly]]; [[Vlad]] shares [[Hartsvale]]'s lore and his own horrifying secrets; the [[Baba Yaga]] wages nightmare warfare on the trek; [[Wild Fury]] is declared Vlad's champion for [[The White Claw|The Krug]]; and a separated [[Icarus]] is smuggled toward the city by [[Garvin]], who believes Icarus is his lost brother Gradley.

## Summary

High above the frozen wastes, a polymorphed [[Icarus]] dive-bombed an illusionary bird, shattering it to reveal [[Vraxis]] — a terrifying **eight-foot-tall cobalt dragon soldier**. Knocked back into his half-orc form, Icarus was spared certain death only because Vraxis was deeply intrigued by his defensive parrot, [[Polly]] — sensing the creature was **no mere bird, but a mysterious fey entity**. Still, Icarus's massive ego took the reins as he stubbornly insisted the lethal soldier was just trying to court him. Their icy saga peaked when Vraxis descended a second time; instead of fleeing, Icarus doubled down on the delusional romance, even inviting the dragon on a date where Vraxis would pay. Thoroughly fed up, Vraxis bypassed the sorcerer's defenses with a potent fear spell, sending him sprinting away in pure terror. After the panic faded, the two endured an excruciatingly awkward walk in the exact same direction, culminating in Vraxis warning Icarus of his hubris and chaotic nature before flying away for good.

Meanwhile, the rest of the party huddled around a campfire with [[Vlad]], a twenty-foot Frost Giant **exiled from [[Hartsvale]]**. Vlad detailed the city's lore — including Lord [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan Ghartovich]]'s ruling style, the Twilight Maiden's ([[Princess Zaria]]) rumored blessed survival at birth thanks to a wish on a shooting star, and a haunting dream-entity known as the [[Baba Yaga]]. Driven by a mysterious arena flyer pointing toward [[Wild Fury]]'s long-lost father, Vlad guided them on a **three-day trek** toward the city. However, the arctic nights quickly devolved into psychic warfare as the Baba Yaga invaded the party's nightmares, dealing waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia to everyone.

The journey grew more chaotic when a massive winter bear ambushed their polar bear mounts. [[Blackjack]] smooth-talked the wild beast into walking away, but the party's own mauled polar bear was left on death's door. [[Wild Fury]] stepped up to mercy-kill their dying mount and custom-fashioned its fur into a "dope" new hooded jacket, causing Vlad to violently vomit. Shortly after, the giant dropped a horrifying double-revelation: he was the **exiled brother of Hartsvale's ruler**, and his definition of "stealing vegetables" actually meant **eating disabled children**. Despite the accidental cannibalism talk, they persuaded him to declare **Wild Fury III as his champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]]** so they could enter Hartsvale, bypassing a gruesome border outpost littered with the self-mutilated victims of the entity's madness along the way.

Far across the wastes, a freezing, isolated [[Icarus]] finally intercepted an armored [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark Industries]] truck convoy, the company logo and motto — **"One World. One Voice."** — written on the side. Fate dealt a cruel and convenient hand: one of the drivers turned out to be [[Garvin]], a half-orc searching for his brother, **Gradley** — *whose body Icarus now inhabits*. Icarus used his theatrical deception skills to prove his 'identity,' going as far as eating a rotting fish. Completely sold, Garvin agreed to smuggle his 'brother' all the way to Hartsvale. The session ended as the truck crested a snowy ridge, revealing the towering, ominous **iron silhouette of Hartsvale** looming ahead.

## Key Developments

- **[[Vraxis]] revealed:** an eight-foot-tall cobalt dragon soldier disguised as an illusionary bird. He spared [[Icarus]] only out of intrigue at [[Polly]] — whom he sensed to be a **mysterious fey entity**, not a bird — and, after routing Icarus with a fear spell, warned him of his hubris and chaotic nature before flying away for good.
- **[[Hartsvale]] lore** from [[Vlad]]: ruled by Lord [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan Ghartovich]]; home to the Twilight Maiden, [[Princess Zaria]], rumored blessed at birth by a wish on a shooting star; haunted by a dream-entity called the [[Baba Yaga]].
- The **[[Baba Yaga]]** invaded the party's nightmares on the trek — waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia for everyone.
- [[Blackjack]] talked a massive winter bear out of a fight; [[Wild Fury]] mercy-killed the party's mauled polar bear and made its fur into a hooded jacket.
- **[[Vlad]]'s double-revelation:** he is the exiled **brother of Hartsvale's ruler**, and his "stealing vegetables" meant eating disabled children.
- [[Wild Fury]] declared **Vlad's champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]]** — the party's way into Hartsvale, past a gruesome border outpost littered with the Baba Yaga's self-mutilated victims. The Krug is the same arena as [[The White Claw]], and the Twilight Maiden [[Princess Zaria]] is the flyer's "maiden."
- **[[Garvin]] and Gradley:** Icarus's reincarnated half-orc body belongs to **Gradley**, the lost brother of [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark]] driver [[Garvin]] — who is now smuggling "his brother" into Hartsvale.
- First sighting of **Hartsvale itself**: a towering, ominous iron silhouette.
