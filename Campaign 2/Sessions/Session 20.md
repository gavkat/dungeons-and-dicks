# Session 20: Twilight

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** On the astral plane, **[[X the Hamster|X]]** — a giant space hamster and **graphic novelist of high-fantasy erotica** — spots an apparition of [[Icarus]], pockets an **IOU** signed by him, and sets course for Faerûn to find his muse; in [[Hartsvale]]'s throne room [[Icarus]] paralyzes a white-fury [[Vlad]], [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] impales the last [[Gnoll Enforcers|gnoll guard]] with [[Dragon Saver]], X crashes through the ceiling, Icarus's **Stormwing Strike** burns away half of **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]** before a surge turns him into a **houseplant**, and [[Rik]] **beheads the tyrant** and drop-kicks the head into the crowd; the mob, now in the **white fury** too, turns on **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]**, and the party smuggles her out as a pterodactyl and two flies; in a tavern suite her crown's **obsidian star** casts a soothing twilight — until she unfolds into the **[[Baba Yaga]]**, a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**, whose nightmares show the party their worst fears and show them **Kholodan's bargain with a falling star and [[The Dark Six]]**; Icarus melts her husk, the [[Immovable Rod]] shatters the **star-phylactery**, and at dawn the **eternal blizzard begins to thaw**.

## Summary

The session opened on the **Astral Plane**, the day before the battle. Drifting through wildspace in an **oversized celestial hamster ball** cluttered with **manuscripts and floating inkwells**, **[[X the Hamster|X]]** — a **giant space hamster** and **graphic novelist of high-fantasy erotica** — spotted the **radiant apparition** of a **pink-haired half-orc sorcerer**: [[Icarus]]. Catching the lingering command to *"fly, boy, fly"* — the words [[The Cloaked Figure|the cloaked figure]] gave Icarus on the astral plane in [[Session 18]] — and pocketing a **drifting IOU signed by the sorcerer**, X set a course for **Faerûn** to find his mysterious muse.

In [[Hartsvale]]'s throne room, the present erupted. An enraged **[[Vlad]]** plummeted from his cage, seized by **the same white fury that had claimed [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]**, while **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]** sneered from within a **globe of invulnerability**. [[Icarus]] **magically paralyzed Vlad**, and [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **impaled the remaining gnoll honor guard** with **[[Dragon Saver]]**. Icarus then **warped Kholodan out of his protective ward** just as **[[X the Hamster|X the Hamster]] crashed through the vaulted ceiling** in his ball, having located his **"magnum opus"**. Capitalizing on the surprise, Icarus unleashed a **Stormwing Strike** of crackling blue lightning that **incinerated half the tyrant's body** — only for **wild magic to turn him into a potted houseplant**. As the revolutionary mob battered down the doors, Kholodan **pleaded for his daughter's life** — but **[[Rik]] executed him with a single greatsword sweep** and **drop-kicked the severed head into the roaring crowd**.

Kholodan's death **freed [[Vlad]]**, but the victory turned bitter when the mob turned on the innocent **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]**, their own eyes **overtaken by the infectious white fury**. About to be swarmed by the people they had liberated, the companions improvised an exit: **Vlad braced his massive frame against the doorway** to hold back the feral crowd, while [[Icarus]] — no longer a pot plant — **polymorphed into a pterodactyl** to fly an **invisible Zarya** to safety, and deployed **fly charms** that turned [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] into **flies** to slip out unnoticed. They regrouped in the **vacant king's suite of a tavern**, where patrons below still **hummed the anthem Wild Fury had commissioned**. Zarya grieved for her people, reflecting that a true ruler should be **a hearth for their kingdom rather than freezing the valley to keep a single candle lit**. Removing her crown, she revealed an **obsidian star at its core** — the relic of a **wish made upon a falling star when her mother died** — and channeled it to cast a **soothing, starlit twilight** across the room, urging the exhausted heroes to rest.

The long rest did not last. The party noticed **Zarya sitting upright, glassy-eyed**, as her **jaw dislocated past her collarbone**, her **spine inverted** and her **skin split into bone needles** — shedding her gown to reveal the **iron-toothed guise of the [[Baba Yaga]]**. In the chaos [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] triggered his **[[Wand of Wonder]]** and **conjured a live rhinoceros** into the already-crammed suite. Slipping between shadows, the Baba Yaga **snuffed out the starlight**, plunging the room into **magical darkness** and a **psychic shroud of paralyzing fear**:

- [[Icarus]] **choked in a stagnant mire**, mocked for his **false godhood and vanity** by **the corpse of his father** ([[Reginald]]'s, by implication — a nightmare, not news).
- [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **suffocated beneath granite**, reliving **his father's crushing death**, while **[[Dragon Saver]] turned to ash** and his **[[Immovable Rod]] clicked into empty air**.
- [[Rik]] sat frozen before apparitions of **his dying wife and son** ([[Marisya]] and [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]) set against **monolithic void-monsters leveling cities**.
- [[X the Hamster|X]] drifted through **freezing wildspace** as his **manuscripts liquefied**, while the **psionic shrieks of Mind Flayers** echoed through the **burning ruins of his obliterated home village**.

Through the tempest, visions exposed **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s past**: the frost giant lord **weeping over his dying wife in childbirth**, **bargaining to a falling star and [[The Dark Six]]** to **birth an avatar of fury**.

Tearing free of the psychic assault, [[Icarus]] — with a **newfound sense of self-worth** — unleashed a **critical conflagration of magical fire** that **melted the [[Baba Yaga]]'s physical husk into slag**. Realizing from the visions that **the crone was a [[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**, the party identified the **obsidian star in Zarya's crown as her phylactery**. Icarus **teleported into the air to seize the falling-star projection**, and the companions brought the **[[Immovable Rod]]** crashing down with shattering force. The star **fractured in an explosive radiant nova**, **breaking the God's tether**. As dawn arrived, **the eternal blizzard finally began to thaw over the valley**.

*The session art shows [[X the Hamster|X]] rocketing through space in a blue-and-gold hamster ball; a pink-feathered, bird-headed [[Icarus]] — [[Polly]] on his shoulder — wreathed in blue lightning against a chain-bound, purple-crackling [[Vlad]]; [[Rik]] swinging his blade at a kneeling, white-haired [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] before the throne; and Icarus pouring a torrent of fire into a clawed, skeletal crone against a starfield.*

## Key Developments

- **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] is dead.** Pulled out of his globe of invulnerability by [[Icarus]] and half-incinerated by a Stormwing Strike, he begged for his daughter's life and was **beheaded by [[Rik]]**, who **drop-kicked the head into the mob**.
- **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] was the [[Baba Yaga]] — and the Baba Yaga was a [[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]].** In the tavern suite her body **inverted into the iron-toothed crone**. The party worked out from the visions that it was a **Vox**, with the **obsidian star in Zarya's crown** as its **phylactery**.
- **Her origin, from the nightmare visions:** Kholodan, **weeping over his wife dying in childbirth**, **bargained with a falling star and [[The Dark Six]] to birth an avatar of fury**. Zarya herself called the star **a wish made upon a falling star when her mother died** — the shooting-star birth rumor [[Vlad]] told in [[Session 14]], given its price.
- **A second Vox falls.** [[Icarus]] **melted the husk to slag**; the [[Immovable Rod]] **shattered the star** in a radiant nova, **breaking the God's tether**. The source does not say which of the Six the God is. (*An avatar "of fury" suggests **The Fury** — an inference, not a statement.*)
- **The thaw.** At dawn, **the eternal blizzard began to thaw over the valley**.
- **[[The White Fury|The white fury]] is infectious.** It held [[Vlad]] until **Kholodan's death freed him**, and then it took **[[Hartsvale]]'s revolutionary mob**, who turned on Zarya.
- **[[Vlad]] is free** — and held the doorway against the mob so the party could escape. His fate after that is not stated.
- **A new party member: [[X the Hamster|X]]**, a giant space hamster and erotic graphic novelist from the Astral Plane, who came to Faerûn chasing **[[Icarus]] as his muse** ("magnum opus") with an **IOU signed by Icarus** in his pocket, and whose **home village was obliterated** amid **Mind Flayers**.
- **[[Icarus]]:** paralyzed Vlad, warped Kholodan out of his ward, **Stormwing Strike**, **turned into a houseplant** (again), **pterodactyl** airlift, **fly charms**, and the fire that killed the Vox. His nightmare — **his father's corpse mocking his false godhood** — broke into a **newfound sense of self-worth**. *(The apparition X saw was the **half-orc** Icarus of his [[Session 18]] astral detour, not the human he has been since [[Session 19]].)*
- **The party's fears, laid bare:** [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] relived **his father's crushing death** with [[Dragon Saver]] in ash and the [[Immovable Rod]] missing again; [[Rik]] saw **[[Marisya]] and [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]] dying** amid **void-monsters leveling cities**.
- **The [[Immovable Rod]] is back in the party's hands** — not left hanging over the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]], as [[Session 18]] implied.
- **[[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]]'s [[Wand of Wonder]] conjured a rhinoceros** into the tavern suite.
- **The last [[Gnoll Enforcers|gnoll honor guard]] is dead**, impaled on [[Dragon Saver]].
