# Session 19: Do You Hear the People Sing?

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** The party grieves as stone and dragon bone entomb **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]**; [[Icarus]], falling dead, is snatched from **five towering silhouettes** by **a familiar voice** that tells him to say hi to [[Polly]], and steps out of golden ash as a **pink-haired human** with a sneeze-triggered **Stormwing** form; a very-much-alive **[[Vraxis]]** reforges the Dragon Slayer into **[[Dragon Saver]]**, freezes [[Rik]]'s parasitic scorpion armor into a crest, shows him **[[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]** and a **tethered [[Marisya]]**, and wakes Polly as a **Chwinga**; the party teleports into [[Hartsvale]]'s slums and starts a **revolution** to *The Ballad of David and the Pussycat*; a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** warns Rik his wife is dying; and in the throne room **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]** drops the cage on **[[Vlad]]**, eyes ablaze with the same **white fury** that took Wild Fury II.

## Summary

On the frozen mountain ledge outside the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]], the companions watched in **stunned grief** as **collapsing stone and dragon bone sealed the entrance**, entombing **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]**. Meanwhile, [[Icarus]]'s **broken body plummeted toward the crags**. His dying consciousness flashed through fractured memories: **[[The Cloaked Figure|the cloaked astral figure]]** urging him to *fly, boy, fly*; **a shooting star**; his **throat torn by the tethered pirate** (his [[Session 1]] death); and **[[Rik]]'s blade severing his neck** in the fighting pits ([[Session 7]]). The fall abruptly ceased in an **ink-black void** where **five towering silhouettes** leaned inches from his face. Before their **talons** struck, an **invisible tether wrenched him into a kaleidoscopic realm**. In its center, an **amber cocoon unraveled into a radiant winged vessel** as **a familiar voice** teased that **continually saving an immortal god was getting embarrassing**, bade him **say hi to [[Polly]]**, and snapped him back to life.

Back in the cold, [[Icarus]]'s **smoking half-orc corpse hit the snow** and **split into golden ash**, from which he stepped out as a **pink-haired human**. After a sharp **sneeze**, **sparking blue lightning** temporarily shifted him into an **avian Stormwing form**. Then **[[Vraxis]]** arrived — alive — with **parting gifts and visions**. He guided [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] into a **memory of ash-choked, dragon-damaged [[Leilon]] five years earlier**, where an **unbloodied Wild Fury II** reminded his son that the **[[Lionshield Coster]] was founded by protectors**, and that their true enemies were **not dragons, but the men in velvet coats who pit mortals and dragons against one another** — before **reshaping the Dragon Slayer sword into [[Dragon Saver]]**.

For [[Rik]], [[Vraxis]] **halted the spread of the parasitic scorpion armor**, condensing it into a **scorpion-plated crest bearing a five-headed dragon sigil over his heart**, then granted a **scrying vision**: his son **[[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]** whittling a **toy soldier** beside his wife **[[Marisya]]**, **bound to a bed and writhing in dark tethering**, while **a stranger comforted the boy outside**. [[Vraxis]] then **awakened [[Polly]]**, revealing the bird to be a **Chwinga — a fey spirit** — who **bestowed a protective charm on [[Icarus]]**. Seeking instant shelter, Icarus impulsively **chugged a potion he misread as a hut**, only to find the scratched label read **"Leomund's Tiny Nut"** — triggering a **scandalous wave of euphoric relief** as Vraxis scolded him for **drinking his private reserve**.

Recognizing that a **frontal assault on [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] and his army was suicide**, the party resolved to **incite a civil uprising**, while [[Vraxis]] **conjured a raging blizzard** as cover and **teleported them into [[Hartsvale]]'s slums**. Walking past **familiar spider-clenched-fist graffiti** to the local tavern, [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] was **swarmed by swooning admirers chanting for "Wild and Furry"** before the **bards he had previously commissioned** struck up their new anthem: ***The Ballad of David and the Pussycat***. Seizing the momentum, the party **stoked the crowd into revolutionary fervor** against the Lord's cruelty. Amid the chaos, [[Rik]] locked eyes with a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** whose **handsome face briefly withered into a scalded husk**, murmuring a haunting warning about **wasting precious time away from his dying wife** — before **vanishing into thin air**.

At dawn, the **armed mob marched behind the bards' rolling stage**, clashing with the **city guard**. Infiltrating through the **catacombs**, the party discovered an **abandoned infirmary** holding the **unburied corpse of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s late wife** and a **journal detailing her fatal childbirth** — the loss that had **driven the Lord to madness and dark bargains**. Breaching the **throne room**, they confronted Kholodan, **ensconced within an impervious protective orb**, and **three elite [[Gnoll Enforcers|gnoll enforcers]] wielding three-headed flails**. When **paralyzing toxins and psychic strikes pinned the frontline**, [[Icarus]] triggered an **erratic wild surge** that turned **the entire party invisible**, enabling a **devastating ambush**. Desperate as his guard crumbled, Kholodan **slammed an overhead lever**: an **iron cage crashed from the ceiling** and **shattered to release [[Vlad]]**, his eyes **ablaze with the same feral white fury that had claimed Wild Fury II**, roaring as the battle tipped into madness.

*The session art shows the teleport arrival through a crackling blue portal beside the graffiti — a glowing-eyed, plague-masked figure in a bow tie and bandolier among the arrivals; [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] in his white fur jacket and a pink-haired [[Icarus]], [[Polly]] on his shoulder, toasting on a tavern table, with a red-hooded figure watching from the back; and the three gnolls before Kholodan's glowing orb, one swinging a flail of skulls.*

## Key Developments

- **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] is entombed.** Stone and **dragon bone** sealed the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]'s entrance as the party watched in grief. The source still does not show him die.
- **[[Icarus]]'s third death did not hold — "a familiar voice" saved him again.** Mid-fall, five **taloned silhouettes** reached for him in a black void; an **invisible tether** pulled him into a **kaleidoscopic realm** (the word used for his [[Session 6]] dream), where an **amber cocoon became a radiant winged vessel** and a voice joked that **saving an immortal god was getting embarrassing** — and told him to **say hi to [[Polly]]**.
- **New body: a pink-haired human.** His half-orc corpse — **Gradley's**, per [[Garvin]] ([[Session 14]]) — **burned to golden ash**, and he stepped out of it human. A **sneeze** of blue lightning briefly turns him into an **avian Stormwing form**.
- **[[Vraxis]] survived the cave-in — and is now helping the party.** He came with gifts and visions, then **conjured a blizzard** and **teleported the party into [[Hartsvale]]** for the uprising.
- **The Coster's real enemy, per Wild Fury II:** **not dragons, but "the men in velvet coats"** who **pit mortals and dragons against one another** — delivered in a vision of [[Leilon]] after the dragon attack, five years ago. The [[Lionshield Coster]] was **founded by protectors**.
- **The Dragon Slayer is now [[Dragon Saver]]** — reshaped by [[Vraxis]] for [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]].
- **[[Rik]]'s parasitic scorpion armor is stopped** — condensed into a **scorpion-plated crest** with a **five-headed dragon sigil** over his heart. (The armor itself is new to this vault.)
- **[[Rik]] has a son: [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]** In [[Vraxis]]'s scrying vision, **[[Marisya]] is bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering** — the first time her curse has been described in the language of **[[The Tethered]]** — while **a stranger comforts the boy outside**.
- **[[Polly]] is a Chwinga** — a fey spirit, **awakened by [[Vraxis]]**, who gave [[Icarus]] a **protective charm**. The fey reading of [[Session 14]] and [[Session 17]] confirmed.
- **"Leomund's Tiny Nut," not "Hut."** The potion [[Icarus]] drank — very likely the lab's ambiguously labelled **"Liomund's Tiny Hut"** of [[Session 18]] — had a **scratched label**, and belonged to [[Vraxis]]'s **private reserve**.
- **[[Hartsvale]] is in revolt.** Wild Fury III is a folk hero there ("Wild and Furry"), with a commissioned anthem — ***The Ballad of David and the Pussycat*** — and the city's slums carry **spider-clenched-fist graffiti** the party recognized.
- **A [[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** — handsome face withering to a **scalded husk** — warned [[Rik]] he is **wasting precious time away from his dying wife**, then vanished.
- **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s motive:** his **wife died in childbirth**; her **unburied corpse** lies in an abandoned infirmary under the castle, and her loss **drove him to madness and dark bargains**.
- **The throne-room fight:** Kholodan inside an **impervious protective orb**; three **[[Gnoll Enforcers|gnoll enforcers]]** with **three-headed flails**; a frontline pinned by **paralyzing toxins and psychic strikes**; and an **invisibility wild surge** from [[Icarus]] that turned it into an ambush.
- **[[Vlad]] has the white fury.** Kholodan's lever dropped the frost giant's cage, and Vlad came out with **the same feral white fury that had claimed Wild Fury II**. The session ends mid-battle.
