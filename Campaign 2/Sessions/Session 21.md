# Session 21: There's something fishy in Brinewater Bay

**Tags:** #session

**In short:** Shattering the obsidian star pulls the party into a shared psychic vision of **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]**'s bargain with six discordant voices ([[The Dark Six]]) to save newborn **[[Princess Zaria|Zarya]]**, the birth of the **[[Baba Yaga]]** curse, the founding of **[[The White Claw|the Krug Arena]]**, and **[[Vraxis]]**'s scapegoating; back in thawing **[[Hartsvale]]**, **[[Polly]]** gifts **[[Icarus]]** a **Boar's Hide Chwinga Charm** and **[[X the Hamster|X]]** frees a concussed **[[Blackjack]]** from the dungeons; X impersonates Gradley to hijack **[[Garvin]]**'s transport convoy — before X turns Garvin invisible and Icarus *Vortex Warps* him out of the moving cab into the snow — and on the three-day drive **[[Rik]]** spots **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger]]** beside a **large wolf**; reaching smog-choked **[[Brinewater Bay]]** beneath a **[[Glimmerspark Industries]] Compound** patrolled by the **[[Mage Guard]]**, X's *Greater Restoration* backfires with necrotic damage to expose **Blackjack as a willing vessel** of the **[[Shard of the Great Devourer]]** and **[[The Great Devourer|its patron]]**, and X translates Rik's **[[Tomb of Tzentak]]** tome — ***[[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]*** — revealing the multi-step cure for **[[Marisya]]**'s **[[The Tethered|'Tethered']]** curse; after trading for **pure zinc** at *Ship Happens* (learning of a **crushed underwater Glimmerspark pipeline** amid rumors of crushed ships and hollowed-out fish) and learning **grave-lotus petals** grow only in the **Feywild**, the party splits for the night — and at the brothel *Catch of the Day*, a **magical compulsion** claims **[[Wild Fury]]** completely.

## Summary

Shattering the obsidian star within [[Princess Zaria|Zarya]]'s crown pulled the party into a harrowing shared psychic vision of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s descent into madness. Amid the frantic clatter of surgical basins, Kholodan's **giantess wife** bled out in childbirth as their unborn infant's pulse withered. Collapsing into the snowdrifts outside, a broken Kholodan saw a star falling in a sickly violet arc and whispered a final plea. The wind died, the snow hung suspended in midair, and **six discordant voices** ([[The Dark Six]]) echoed, offering to **rekindle one spark while claiming the other for the dark**. Choosing his daughter, Kholodan retrieved the pulsing **obsidian star-stone** from its mountain crater, embedded it into **his late wife's crown**, and placed it upon newborn Zarya's brow. Grief rotted into paranoia: he **sealed his love's rotting corpse in the birthing suite**, plagued by nightmares of peasant mobs fusing into a skeletal monster to devour Zarya. One night he awoke to find his daughter **clinging to the ceiling like an arachnid**, neck contorted, speaking in an ancient tongue — before she woke **completely oblivious**. As the curse bled into [[Hartsvale]] through shared nightmares of the **[[Baba Yaga]]**, Kholodan **established the brutal [[The White Claw|Krug Arena]] to vent the people's fury** and let the dark voices twist the soaring dragon **[[Vraxis]]** into his scapegoat.

Snapping back to reality as **[[Polly]]** gifted **[[Icarus]]** a **Boar's Hide Chwinga Charm** — triggering a memory of **[[Gearsley]]** teaching him about the resilience of boar hide — the party watched the cursed winter thaw over [[Hartsvale]]'s blood-soaked streets. Descending into the dungeons, **[[X the Hamster|X]]** gnawed through iron chains to rescue their concussed companion, **[[Blackjack]]**, who still bore the pulsing **[[Shard of the Great Devourer]]**. Seeking to escape the continent and return to **[[Leilon]]**, the party targeted a transport convoy run by **[[Garvin]]**, the half-orc brother of Icarus's late half-orc persona, Gradley. Approaching the docks without him, Garvin and his posse were skeptical of the party's flimsy excuses that Gradley was tied up with "post-revolution admin" and refused to let them hitch a ride unless Gradley appeared by 10:00 AM the next morning. The next morning, X cast *Disguise Self* to impersonate Gradley, fooling the wholesome trucker into letting them ride. Once the illusion faded, X turned Garvin **invisible** and Icarus cast ***Vortex Warp*** to teleport him **ninety feet out of the moving cab into the snow**, leaving **blood splatters beneath the wheel** moments later. During the grueling three-day drive — which left X and Icarus exhausted — **[[Rik]]** spotted the familiar **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|Crimson-Hooded Stranger]]** watching them beside a **large wolf**, unnoticed by the others. Further discussions reminded Rik of the untranslated tome recovered from the **[[Tomb of Tzentak]]** ([[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]) that might cure his dying wife, **[[Marisya]]**.

Arriving at **[[Brinewater Bay]]**, the party found the coastal village choked by the sulfurous smog of the overshadowing **[[Glimmerspark Industries]] Compound**, patrolled by the **[[Mage Guard]]** (credited in the recap with previously stealing their wyvern, **[[Dave]]** — tied to the [[Session 13]] ambush in which a cornered Mage Guard called in a "Code Black" abductor). X disguised himself as Garvin to bluff past the checkpoint before heading to ***The Codfather*** tavern. There, X's attempt to cast ***Greater Restoration*** on [[Blackjack]] **backfired with necrotic damage**, revealing that **Blackjack is willingly acting as the amulet's vessel and accepting [[The Great Devourer|the patron]]**. Translating Rik's tome — ***[[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]*** — X learned that curing [[Marisya]] of **[[The Tethered|'the Tethered' curse]]** requires a precise sequence: rare components (**black wormwood**, **grave-lotus petals**, and **vitriol of zinc**), followed by a **silver-needle siphon** into a bath of **goat's milk**, **powdered pearls**, and **sacramental wine**, and finished with ***Greater Restoration***.

Exploring [[Brinewater Bay]]'s port amidst rumors of **ships crushed in dead-calm waters** and **fish pulled up completely hollowed out**, the party bypassed an unhelpful stop at ***Old Salty's General Store*** to visit ***Ship Happens***. For **ten gold** and a **late-night rendezvous with [[Rik]] while [[X the Hamster|X]] watched**, the **half-orc blacksmith** traded **pure zinc** — noting it required **dragonfire or infernal flames** to refine — and revealed that an **underwater [[Glimmerspark Industries|Glimmerspark]] pipeline had been crushed by something huge**. After learning at ***Kelp Yourself*** that **grave-lotus petals only grow in the Feywild**, X took [[Blackjack]] to rest at ***The Codfather Part II***. The rest of the party visited a brothel called ***Catch of the Day***. Growing suspicious of the alluring women inside, they tried to leave, triggering a **magical compulsion**: **[[Rik]]** and **[[Icarus]]** resisted, while **[[Wild Fury]]** succumbed completely to the enchantment.

*The session art shows a blue-skinned, crowned [[Princess Zaria|Zarya]] clinging upside down to ceiling rafters with glowing eyes and a contorted neck; a smiling [[X the Hamster|X]] and pink-haired [[Icarus]] (with [[Polly]] on his shoulder) riding in the wooden cab of the stolen transport across the snow; and a hooded figure by candlelight studying the open leather-bound tome titled* Anatomia Maledicti.

- **Origin of [[The White Claw|the Krug Arena]] and [[Vraxis]]'s scapegoating:** as the [[Baba Yaga]] curse bled into [[Hartsvale]] through shared nightmares, Kholodan **built the Krug Arena to vent the people's fury** and let the dark voices twist **[[Vraxis]]** into his scapegoat.
- **[[Blackjack]] rescued — and exposed as a willing vessel:** [[X the Hamster|X]] gnawed through iron chains in [[Hartsvale]]'s dungeons to free the concussed Blackjack, still wearing the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]]. At *The Codfather*, X's *Greater Restoration* **backfired with necrotic damage**, revealing Blackjack is **willingly acting as the amulet's vessel and accepting [[The Great Devourer|the patron]]**.
- **[[Polly]]'s gift & [[Gearsley]]'s memory:** Polly gifted [[Icarus]] a **Boar's Hide Chwinga Charm**, triggering a memory of Gearsley teaching him about the resilience of boar hide.
