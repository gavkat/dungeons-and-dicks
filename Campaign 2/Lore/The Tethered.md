# The Tethered

**Tags:** #Lore #Mystery

---

## Overview
People falling mysteriously ill since the [[Phandelver#The Forge of Spells|Forge of Spells]] was reactivated — the dark undercurrent of the new age. Likely connected to the whispers of people changing and *eyes turning black* — as seen in the pirates at [[Leilon#The Crow's Nest|The Crow's Nest]] ([[Session 1]]) and possibly the dominated [[Sauriv]] ([[Session 3]]).

## Session History

### Session 1
- [[Halia]] warned [[Wild Fury]] of the tethered; black-eyed pirates attacked that same night.

### Session 19
- [[Icarus]] recalled his [[Session 1]] death as his **throat torn by the tethered pirate** — evidently one of the black-eyed Crow's Nest pirates, which would make them tethered.
- [[Vraxis]]'s scrying vision showed [[Rik]]'s wife **[[Marisya]] bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering** — the first sign her curse is this affliction.
