# The Keeper

**Tags:** #Lore #WorldBuilding

---

## Overview
A deity (or power) honored at the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] — its followers interred there, its name carried by the [[Medji-Tzentak]] ("Sons of the Keeper") and the tomb's "Hallway of the Keeper."

**Confirmed as one of [[The Dark Six]]** ([[Session 16]]), when **Wild Fury II** named the full roster: The Mischief, **the Keeper**, the Devourer, the Fury, the Traveler, and the Shadow. That casts the tomb in a new light: [[Tzentak]] — a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]** who struck a deal with the Six — lies in a funerary complex built around the worship of one of them.
