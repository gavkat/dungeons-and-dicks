# Brinewater Bay

**Tags:** #Location #Campaign2

---

## Overview
A smog-choked coastal port village reached after a three-day drive from [[Hartsvale]] as the party headed toward [[Leilon]] ([[Session 21]]). The village is overshadowed by a **[[Glimmerspark Industries]] Compound** that fills the air with **sulfurous smog**, and its checkpoints and streets are patrolled by the **[[Mage Guard]]**.

Beneath its industrial haze, something is hunting in the bay — and something enchanted lurks in its brothel.

## Notable Locations
- **[[Glimmerspark Industries]] Compound** — an industrial complex overshadowing the village and blanketing it in sulfurous smog. An **underwater Glimmerspark pipeline** in the bay was recently **crushed by something huge**.
- ***The Codfather*** — a local tavern where the party regrouped after bluffing past the [[Mage Guard]] checkpoint; here [[X the Hamster|X]]'s *Greater Restoration* on [[Blackjack]] backfired with necrotic damage and X translated ***[[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]***.
- ***The Codfather Part II*** — an inn or sister establishment where [[X the Hamster|X]] took [[Blackjack]] to rest.
- ***Old Salty's General Store*** — an unhelpful shop the party bypassed while searching for cure ingredients.
- ***Ship Happens*** — a smithy run by a **half-orc blacksmith**, who traded **pure zinc** for ten gold and a late-night rendezvous with [[Rik]] (while [[X the Hamster|X]] watched), noted that refining it requires **dragonfire or infernal flames**, and revealed the crushed underwater Glimmerspark pipeline.
- ***Kelp Yourself*** — a local shop where the party learned that **grave-lotus petals** only grow in the **Feywild**.
- ***Catch of the Day*** — a brothel staffed by **alluring women**. When [[Rik]], [[Icarus]], and [[Wild Fury]] grew suspicious and tried to leave, they triggered a **magical compulsion** — Rik and Icarus resisted, while Wild Fury succumbed completely.

## Rumors & Mysteries
- **Ships crushed in dead-calm waters** and **fish pulled up completely hollowed out** — paired with the **crushed underwater Glimmerspark pipeline**, pointing to something huge beneath the bay.
- **The enchantment at *Catch of the Day*** — the alluring women triggered a magical compulsion when the party tried to leave, leaving [[Wild Fury]] completely enthralled.

## Session History
