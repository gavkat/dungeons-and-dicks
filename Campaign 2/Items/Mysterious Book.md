# Mysterious Book (*Anatomia Maledicti*)

**Tags:** #Item #Lore #Campaign2

> **Identified & Translated ([[Session 21]]):** The untranslated tome recovered from the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] in [[Session 11]] is titled ***Anatomia Maledicti***. Wikilinks to `[[Anatomia Maledicti]]` resolve here.

---

## Overview
A leather-bound tome recovered near where the genie [[Kaza-Rul]] was jarred in the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] ([[Session 11]]). Unexamined for ten sessions, it was remembered by [[Rik]] on the drive out of [[Hartsvale]] and translated by [[X the Hamster|X]] at [[Brinewater Bay]]'s *The Codfather* tavern ([[Session 21]]).

The book lays out the cure for **[[The Tethered|'the Tethered' curse]]** afflicting Rik's wife, **[[Marisya]]**:
1. Gather three rare components: **black wormwood**, **grave-lotus petals** (which grow only in the **Feywild**, per *Kelp Yourself*), and **vitriol of zinc** (the party obtained **pure zinc** at *Ship Happens*, which requires **dragonfire or infernal flames** to refine).
2. Perform a **silver-needle siphon** into a bath of **goat's milk**, **powdered pearls**, and **sacramental wine**.
3. Finish the ritual with ***Greater Restoration***.
