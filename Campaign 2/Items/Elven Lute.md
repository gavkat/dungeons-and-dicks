# Elven Lute

**Tags:** #Item #Lore

---

## Overview
An **ancient elven lute**, unnamed in the telling — and the **source of the fairy bard [[Dew Dew Bun]]'s powers** ([[Session 16]]).

Its provenance is the reveal that matters: the lute was originally a **gift from [[Tymora]] to her sister [[Beshaba]]** — two of the **seven leaders of [[The Ilefarn|Ilefarn]]**, before Tymora's voluntary departure and before the remaining six became **[[The Dark Six]]**.

Uncovering that chain is what turned **Wild Fury II**'s search for a missing celebrity into a hunt for the Dark Six — and what ultimately stranded him in [[Hartsvale]].

## Current Whereabouts
**Unknown.** The lute's fate after [[Dew Dew Bun]]'s disappearance has never been stated.
