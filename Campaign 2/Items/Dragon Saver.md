**Tags:** #Item

---

## Overview
The sword formerly called the **Dragon Slayer** — **reshaped into Dragon Saver** by [[Vraxis]] in [[Session 19]] as part of his gifts to [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]], and now Wild Fury III's — he **impaled [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s last gnoll guard** with it ([[Session 20]]).

The reforging came at the end of a vision Vraxis guided him into: **ash-choked, dragon-damaged [[Leilon]] five years earlier**, where an **unbloodied Wild Fury II** told his son that the **[[Lionshield Coster]] was founded by protectors**, and that **their true enemies were not dragons, but the men in velvet coats who pit mortals and dragons against one another**. A dragon remade the son's dragon-slaying blade straight after. The new name is the lesson.

The vault has no earlier record of the Dragon Slayer by name; what the reshaping changed beyond the name is not stated.
