# Flame Tongue Whip

**Tags:** #Item #Weapon

---

## Overview
A flaming whip snatched from the altar of the [[Tomb of Tzentak]]'s trophy room by [[Blackjack]] and tossed to [[Rik]], who used it to carve through the tomb's mummies ([[Session 10]]). Rik's signature weapon since.

## Current Whereabouts
**Stolen.** During the party's extended rest in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 17]]), an **unseen thief** slipped into camp past [[Gearsley]]'s watch and took the whip **straight out of [[Rik]]'s holster**, along with the party's gold, all their rations, and one of Gearsley's guns.

The thieves were unmasked in [[Session 18]] as **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]**, found **playing with the party's stolen gear** in [[Vraxis]]'s archive. [[Gearsley]]'s gun was recovered there; **nothing has been said about the whip**, and the cave came down on top of it.
