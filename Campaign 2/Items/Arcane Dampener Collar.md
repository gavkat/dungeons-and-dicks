# Arcane Dampener Collar

**Tags:** #Item

---

## Overview
An **arcane dampener collar**, **gifted by [[Glimmerspark Industries]]** and handed to the party by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] ([[Session 16]]) as the tool for taking **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** **alive**.

**It was never used on the Devil.** In [[Session 18]] the party tried to ambush **[[Vraxis]]** with it, having decided the cobalt dragon was "the devil in the ice" — and were wrong. Minutes later [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **snapped it onto his own father** to break the **supernatural bloodlust** that had seized him again, and it **worked**. [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s monster-catching device is now around **Wild Fury II**'s neck, sealed in a collapsed mountain.

Its presence says two things at once: that Kholodan expects the Devil's threat to be **magical**, and that [[Glimmerspark Industries]] — the industrial power whose convoys, ID passes, and prototype [[Sending Phone|phones]] keep turning up around this party — is **supplying a frost giant lord's monster-hunt**.

## Session History

### Session 16
- Given to the party in [[Hartsvale]]'s throne room as part of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain: capture [[The Devil in the Ice]] alive in the [[Spine of the World]], and win the party's freedom, the voiding of [[Vlad]]'s execution, and **Wild Fury II**'s liberation.

### Session 18
- **Used in a failed ambush on [[Vraxis]]**, whom the party had convinced themselves was **"the devil in the ice," aka the [[Baba Yaga]]**. Vraxis scoffed at the insult.
- **Snapped onto [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** by [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] during the chaos of Vraxis's attack on [[Icarus]] — **breaking the supernatural bloodlust** that had taken his father again.
- Went down **with Wild Fury II** in the cave-in.
