# Ricky Jr.

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
**[[Rik]]'s son** — never mentioned in the vault before [[Session 19]], when he appeared in a **scrying vision** granted by [[Vraxis]]: a boy **whittling a toy soldier** beside his mother, **[[Marisya]]**, who lay **bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering**.

## Role in Campaign
He raises the stakes of Rik's whole quest. The cure Rik has chased since [[Session 1]] is no longer just for a wife — there is a child at that bedside. In the same vision **a stranger comforted the boy outside**; who that stranger is, the source does not say. The same night in [[Hartsvale]], a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** warned Rik he was **wasting precious time away from his dying wife** — whether the two strangers are one is unstated.

## Session History

### Session 19
- Seen in [[Vraxis]]'s **scrying vision** for [[Rik]], **whittling a toy soldier** beside his bedridden, tethered mother [[Marisya]], while **a stranger comforted him outside**.

### Session 20
- Appeared **dying** beside [[Marisya]] in [[Rik]]'s [[Baba Yaga]]-born nightmare, amid **void-monsters leveling cities** — a fear-vision, not news of him.
