# Phlumphi

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
A friendly, floating **flumph from the Feywild** — a drifting, tendrilled jellyfish of a creature wreathed in swirling arcane light — **accidentally manifested by [[Icarus]]'s chaotic magic** on the crest of the [[Spine of the World]] ([[Session 17]]). Cheerful, brief, and entirely unbothered by the blizzard.

## Role in Campaign
Phlumphi exists to deliver three lines and vanish, and all three matter:

1. **The legendary [[Icarus]] is celebrated across the fae realm as an immortal god.** Icarus declared himself an "Immortal God" in the void before his rebirth ([[Session 8]]) — the Feywild appears to have taken him at his word.
2. **[[Polly]] was recognized on sight.** Spotting the parrot perched on the sorcerer's shoulder, Phlumphi noted that **the two had successfully found each other** — implying the meeting was expected, and watched for.
3. **The news had to be reported back.** To whom was never said. Phlumphi faded out of existence immediately afterwards.

Taken together, the flumph is the strongest confirmation yet of [[Vraxis]]'s read on [[Polly]] as **no mere bird, but a fey entity** ([[Session 14]]) — and the first hint that something in the Feywild has been keeping score on [[Icarus]].
