# Garvin

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
A wholesome half-orc driver in an armored [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark Industries]] transport convoy crossing the frozen wastes. He searched for his brother, **Gradley** — the half-orc whose body [[Icarus]] inhabited from [[Session 8]] until it split into golden ash in [[Session 19]].

## Role in Campaign
Fate dealt a cruel and convenient hand ([[Session 14]]): when a freezing, isolated [[Icarus]] intercepted Garvin's convoy, Icarus used his theatrical deception skills to prove his 'identity' as Gradley — going as far as eating a rotting fish. Completely sold, Garvin agreed to **smuggle his 'brother' all the way to [[Hartsvale]]**.

In [[Session 21]], the party targeted Garvin's convoy again to escape [[Hartsvale]]. After [[X the Hamster|X]] impersonated Gradley with *Disguise Self* to get them aboard and the illusion faded, X turned Garvin **invisible** and Icarus ***Vortex Warped*** him **ninety feet out of the moving cab into the snow**, leaving **blood splatters beneath the wheel** moments later. Whether Garvin survived is unstated. X later wore Garvin's face to bluff past the [[Mage Guard]] checkpoint at [[Brinewater Bay]].

## Session History

### Session 14
- Intercepted by [[Icarus]] while driving in a GlimmerSpark convoy (company motto on the side: *"One World. One Voice."*).
- Convinced that Icarus is his lost brother Gradley; agreed to smuggle him into [[Hartsvale]].
- His truck crested a snowy ridge as the session ended, revealing Hartsvale's towering iron silhouette.

### Session 15
- His convoy delivered "Gradley" to [[Hartsvale]]; disguised with a soot mustache, a [[Glimmerspark Industries|GlimmerSpark]] ID pass, and a jacket, [[Icarus]] slipped past the loading-dock guards into the city.

### Session 21
- Approached at [[Hartsvale]]'s docks by the party seeking a ride back toward [[Leilon]]; he and his posse were skeptical of their flimsy excuses that Gradley was tied up with *"post-revolution admin"* and refused to let them ride unless Gradley appeared by 10:00 AM the next morning.
- Fooled the next morning by [[X the Hamster|X]] casting ***Disguise Self*** to impersonate Gradley, letting the party climb aboard.
- Once the illusion faded, X turned Garvin **invisible** and [[Icarus]] cast ***Vortex Warp*** to teleport him **ninety feet out of the moving cab into the snow**, leaving **blood splatters beneath the wheel** moments later (his survival or death is not explicitly stated).
- X later disguised himself as Garvin to bluff the stolen transport past the [[Mage Guard]] checkpoint at [[Brinewater Bay]].
