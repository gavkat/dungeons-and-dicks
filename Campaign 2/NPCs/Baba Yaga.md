# Baba Yaga

**Tags:** #character #npc #Antagonist #Mystery #Campaign2

> **Destroyed ([[Session 20]]).** The Baba Yaga was **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]** — a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]** born of [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain with **a falling star and [[The Dark Six]]**, its phylactery the **obsidian star** in her crown. [[Icarus]] melted the husk; the party shattered the star.

## Description
A haunting **dream-entity** of the frozen north, spoken of in [[Hartsvale]]'s lore by [[Vlad]] ([[Session 14]]). It invades sleepers' nightmares — psychic warfare that deals **waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia**. Its true nature, unknown for six sessions, was revealed in [[Session 20]]: the **iron-toothed crone** wearing the body of [[Hartsvale]]'s Twilight Maiden.

## Role in Campaign
The arctic nights of the party's three-day trek toward [[Hartsvale]] devolved into psychic warfare as the Baba Yaga invaded everyone's nightmares. Its madness has a body count: the gruesome **border outpost** the party bypassed on the way into Hartsvale was littered with the **self-mutilated victims** of the entity's influence.

## Session History

### Session 14
- Named in [[Vlad]]'s telling of [[Hartsvale]] lore as a haunting dream-entity.
- Invaded the party's nightmares night after night on the trek, dealing waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia to everyone.
- Its madness evidenced by the self-mutilated victims littering the border outpost near Hartsvale.

### Session 16
- Invaded the dying party as a **single harrowing shared nightmare** while they lay near death after the arena — the first time its haunting is described as **shared** rather than individual. They **survived** it and woke in an infirmary, saved by [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]].
- **A rival explanation surfaced:** [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] blamed [[Hartsvale]]'s nightmares not on the Baba Yaga but on **"[[The Devil in the Ice]]"** — the thing he sent the party to cage. Either he is wrong, lying, or the two are connected.

### Session 18
- **The party has collapsed the two names into one.** They ambushed [[Vraxis]] with the [[Arcane Dampener Collar]] convinced he was **"the devil in the ice", aka "Baba Yaga"** — treating the dream-entity and Kholodan's quarry as the same thing, on no evidence anyone has stated.
- **[[Vraxis]] pointed the other way.** He **scoffed at the insult** and declared the mountain **merely echoed nightmares bred within [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s own castle** — which puts the source of the haunting in **[[Hartsvale]]**, not the [[Spine of the World]], and makes Kholodan's story look like misdirection.

### Session 20
- **Revealed in the tavern suite.** [[Princess Zaria|Zarya]], glassy-eyed mid-rest, **dislocated her jaw past her collarbone**, **inverted her spine** and **split her skin into bone needles**, shedding her gown to reveal the **iron-toothed guise of the Baba Yaga**.
- **Snuffed out the starlight**, plunging the room into **magical darkness** and a **psychic shroud of paralyzing fear**: [[Icarus]] in a mire, mocked by his father's corpse; [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] beneath granite, reliving his father's death; [[Rik]] before his dying wife and son and city-leveling void-monsters; [[X the Hamster|X]] in freezing wildspace amid Mind Flayer shrieks. The nightmare also showed **[[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] bargaining to a falling star and [[The Dark Six]] to birth an avatar of fury**.
- **Destroyed.** [[Icarus]]'s **critical conflagration** melted its **physical husk into slag**; the party, realizing it was a **[[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**, identified the crown's **obsidian star** as its **phylactery** and **shattered it with the [[Immovable Rod]]** in a radiant nova, **breaking the God's tether**. At dawn the **eternal blizzard began to thaw**.
- **By implication, settles the [[Session 16]]–[[Session 18]] argument:** the source doesn't say so in words, but if the nightmare-crone was the princess, [[Vraxis]] was right — they were **bred within Kholodan's own castle**, not by [[The Devil in the Ice]].
