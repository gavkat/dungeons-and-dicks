# Marisya

**Tags:** #character #npc #QuestTarget

## Description
[[Rik]]'s wife — ailing under **[[The Tethered|'the Tethered' curse]]** (confirmed in [[Session 21]]) — and mother of his son **[[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]]** ([[Session 19]]). In [[Vraxis]]'s scrying vision she lay **bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering**.

## Role in Campaign
The reason Rik adventures. The cure lead from [[Riptide]] pointed to [[Anauroch]] and the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] — and in [[Session 21]], the untranslated tome recovered from that tomb (***[[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]***) finally paid off. Translating it, [[X the Hamster|X]] confirmed she suffers from **[[The Tethered|'the Tethered' curse]]** and uncovered the cure: rare components (**black wormwood**, **grave-lotus petals** that grow only in the **Feywild**, and **vitriol of zinc**), followed by a **silver-needle siphon** into a bath of **goat's milk**, **powdered pearls**, and **sacramental wine**, finished with ***Greater Restoration***. **Time is short ([[Session 19]]):** a **[[The Crimson-Hooded Stranger|crimson-hooded stranger]]** in [[Hartsvale]] called her Rik's **dying wife**.

## Session History

### Session 1
- Her illness and the [[Anauroch]] cure lead introduced.

### Session 5

### Session 19

### Session 20
- Appeared, **dying** beside their son, in [[Rik]]'s [[Baba Yaga]]-born nightmare — set against **monolithic void-monsters leveling cities**. A fear-vision, not news of her.

### Session 21
- **Confirmed afflicted by [[The Tethered|'the Tethered' curse]]:** [[X the Hamster|X]] translated [[Rik]]'s [[Tomb of Tzentak]] tome, ***[[Mysterious Book|Anatomia Maledicti]]***, revealing the multi-step cure ritual (black wormwood, Feywild-only grave-lotus petals, vitriol of zinc, a silver-needle siphon into goat's milk, powdered pearls, and sacramental wine, and *Greater Restoration*). The party acquired **pure zinc** at [[Brinewater Bay]]'s *Ship Happens* as their first step.
