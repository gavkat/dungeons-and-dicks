# Archibald

**Tags:** #character #npc #Campaign2

## Description
A **goblin adventurer**, found **frozen** beneath a **towering icicle** in the **petrified forest of blue ice and basalt** below the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 18]]) — already dead when the [[Boner Bros]] found him. He is known entirely through his corpse and a **letter to his brother Icey** found with it.

## Role in Campaign
Archibald is the party's first evidence that **someone else came hunting "[[The Devil in the Ice|the Devil in the Ice]]" first** — and did not come back. His letter establishes three things:

1. He **petrified the campsite's yeti with a stolen wand** — solving the mystery of the **nine-foot petrified yeti** the party found in the right corridor and [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] beheaded ([[Session 17]]).
2. He was **hunting the Devil in the Ice** — so the thing has a reputation beyond [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s throne room.
3. His motive was to **cure his brothers' afflictions**. What those afflictions are, and how the Devil was supposed to cure them, is unsaid.

The **crooked [[Wand of Wonder]]** recovered from him passed to [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]], who attuned to it — and whom [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] told had inherited a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard**. Whether that wand is also the "stolen wand" of the letter is never spelled out.

> **Icey.** The letter is addressed to Archibald's brother **Icey** — the same name as the **Goblin** gladiator [[Krug Gladiators|Icey]] who trained beside [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] in the [[The White Claw|Krug]] and died in the second wave of the trials ([[Session 15]]). The source never links them; a goblin named Icey with an afflicted brother sits uncomfortably close to a goblin named Icey dying in [[Hartsvale]]'s arena.
