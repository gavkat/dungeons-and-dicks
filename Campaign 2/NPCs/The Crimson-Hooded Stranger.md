# The Crimson-Hooded Stranger

**Tags:** #character #npc #Mystery #Campaign2

> Not to be confused with **[[The Cloaked Figure]]** (the astral plane) or **[[The Ashen Figure]]** (the [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black"). Nothing in the source connects them.

## Description
A **crimson-hooded stranger** in a [[Hartsvale]] slum tavern whose **handsome face briefly withered into a scalded husk** as [[Rik]] locked eyes with him ([[Session 19]]). He **vanished into thin air** moments later. The session art shows a **red-hooded figure** watching from the back of the cheering tavern.

## Role in Campaign
He said one thing, and it was aimed squarely at Rik: a **haunting warning about wasting precious time away from his dying wife**, [[Marisya]].

It landed hours after [[Vraxis]]'s scrying vision showed Marisya **bound to a bed, writhing in dark tethering**, with **a stranger comforting [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]] outside** — so he knows about Rik's wife, and possibly more. Who he is, what the scalded face means, and whether he is the stranger at the boy's side are all open.
