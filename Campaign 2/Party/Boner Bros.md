# The Boner Bros

**Tags:** #Faction #Party

---

## Overview
The party. Founded as the bond between [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] ([[Session 2]]) over the wrestling hero "Bonecrusher," and grown into the collective name for the whole adventuring crew.

## Members
- [[Wild Fury]] — divine warrior; father found, then **entombed** under the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 18]]–[[Session 19]]); given [[Dragon Saver]] by [[Vraxis]] ([[Session 19]]); his wyvern [[Dave]] was abducted ([[Session 13]]).
- [[Icarus]] — wild-magic noble; three times dead, currently a **pink-haired human** with a sneeze-triggered **Stormwing** form ([[Session 19]]); butler [[Gearsley]].
- [[Blackjack]] — thief turned **Shepherd** of [[The Great Devourer]]; bears the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]]; held hostage in [[Hartsvale]] by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] since [[Session 16]]; Kholodan is now dead ([[Session 20]]), and Blackjack still unaccounted for.
- [[Rik]] — pit champion seeking a cure for [[Marisya]]; wears the [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]] — which **unsealed the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]'s hidden stairway** ([[Session 18]]) — and wielded the [[Flame Tongue Whip]] until it was stolen ([[Session 17]]); bears a **scorpion-plated crest** over his heart and has a son, [[Ricky Jr|Ricky Jr.]] ([[Session 19]]); **beheaded [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]** ([[Session 20]]).
- [[X the Hamster|X]] — giant space hamster and erotic graphic novelist who crashed in from the Astral Plane chasing [[Icarus]] as his muse ([[Session 20]]).

## Companions
- [[Gearsley]] — butler, driver, dead-raiser, heart-shooter.
- [[Dave]] — wyvern; abducted by [[The Ashen Figure]] in a [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black" ([[Session 13]]).
- [[Polly]] — teleporting parrot, revealed as a **Chwinga** fey spirit ([[Session 19]]); calls Icarus "daddy."

## Known History
- Formed aboard [[The Tempest's Temptation]] ([[Session 2]]); survived the [[Sovereign Prophet]] and being swallowed by [[The Great Devourer]] ([[Session 6]]), the [[Itju-Sha]] ([[Session 7]]–[[Session 8]]), and the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] ([[Session 10]]–[[Session 11]]); destroyed Krampus at [[Holly Hollow]] ([[Session 12]]).
- Trekked three days through the frozen wastes toward [[Hartsvale]] guided by the exiled Frost Giant [[Vlad]], harried nightly by the [[Baba Yaga]]'s nightmares ([[Session 14]]). Entry to the city secured by [[Wild Fury]]'s declaration as Vlad's champion at [[The White Claw|The Krug]] — while a separated [[Icarus]] is smuggled in by [[Garvin]].
- Split at [[Hartsvale]]'s gates ([[Session 15]]): [[Blackjack]] and the warforged [[Gearsley]] were turned away to an eastern outpost, [[Icarus]] was captured by [[Kenky]], and [[Rik]] and [[Wild Fury]] fought the Krug trials — where Wild Fury's father was revealed as the champion **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**.
- **Wiped out in the arena and brought back** ([[Session 16]]): the Maiden's Champion cut down [[Icarus]], [[Rik]], and [[Wild Fury]], and only **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]'s secret intervention** saved them. Now bound by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain and marching on the **[[Spine of the World]]** to cage **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** — with **[[Gearsley]] returned** and **Wild Fury II** in the party, but **[[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]] held hostage** in [[Hartsvale]].
- **Into the [[Spine of the World]]** ([[Session 17]]): fought off the [[Ice Golems|ice golems]] that had been the shadowy figures in the snow, banished a **colossal ice titan**, met the Feywild flumph [[Phlumphi]], and descended into the **[[Dragon-Skull Cave]]** — where a **gold illusion** nearly dissolved three of them in acid and an **unseen thief** robbed the camp of its gold, rations, one of [[Gearsley]]'s guns, and [[Rik]]'s [[Flame Tongue Whip]].
- **Down a man and a mountain** ([[Session 18]]): below the mural they found the frozen goblin [[Archibald]], a [[Wand of Wonder]], an alchemical laboratory and the archive of **[[Vraxis]]** — whom they collared by mistake, believing him **[[The Devil in the Ice|the Devil in the Ice]]**. Vraxis **killed [[Icarus]]** and brought the cavern down; **[[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]]** caught the collapsing threshold on his back so his son could run, and was **sealed inside**. They ran for the threshold with [[Gearsley]]'s **[[Thoughts & Prayers|pistols]]** and nothing else they had been sent for.
- **Revolution** ([[Session 19]]): [[Icarus]] came back from the dead a **pink-haired human**; [[Vraxis]], alive and suddenly generous, handed out gifts and visions and **teleported them into [[Hartsvale]]**, where they **raised the slums against [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]** to the tune of ***The Ballad of David and the Pussycat*** — and fought their way into his throne room, only for him to loose a white-fury-maddened [[Vlad]] on them.
- **Regicide and a Vox** ([[Session 20]]): joined by the astral space hamster [[X the Hamster|X]], they broke [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s throne room — [[Rik]] took his head — then rescued **[[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]]** from a mob gone feral with the **[[The White Fury|white fury]]**, only for her to become the **[[Baba Yaga]]** in their tavern suite. They survived her nightmares, melted her husk, and **shattered her obsidian-star phylactery** with the [[Immovable Rod]] — their **second [[Vox of the Dark Six|Vox]]**. At dawn the blizzard began to thaw.
