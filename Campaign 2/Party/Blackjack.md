# Jack "Blackjack" Blackwood

**Tags:** #character #pc #BottomFeeders #Shepherd

### Character Concept

You are **Jack "Blackjack" Blackwood**, an opportunist, thief, and showman — the **Shepherd** of [[The Great Devourer]], revealed in [[Session 21]] to be **willingly acting as the amulet's vessel and accepting the patron**.

**Core Traits:**
- A flair for the dramatic: staged car accidents, trap-gauntlet sprints screaming "Jack-Attack!", and tall tales about a "sea girlfriend" who is definitely, totally real.
- Bears the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]], which beats in time with his heart. Where others rotted, he became a vessel — and when [[X the Hamster|X]] tried to cast *Greater Restoration* on him in [[Session 21]], it **backfired with necrotic damage**, exposing that Blackjack is **willingly** serving as vessel and accepting [[The Great Devourer|his patron]].
- Powers gained beneath the sea: the Fathomless Plunge and spectral tentacles ([[Session 9]]).
- The [[Sovereign Prophet]] hunts the amulet's thief — him — and [[Icarus]] has been ordered to deliver that thief.
- Formerly **held hostage** in [[Hartsvale]] by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] ([[Session 16]]–[[Session 21]]); **rescued concussed** from the castle dungeons by [[X the Hamster|X]] after Kholodan's fall ([[Session 21]]).

## Session History

### Session 1
- Faked being hit by [[Gearsley]]'s car to steal [[Icarus]]'s [[Sending Phone]]. Joined the [[Bottom Feeders]].

### Session 2
- Agreed to the [[Polly]] heist on his own terms (and the return of the phone). Shadowed [[Captain Siren Shellsword]] and [[Skum]] below deck, overhearing their plotting.

### Session 3
- Overheard the Captain plotting a "reward." After the fight with the dominated [[Sauriv]], the cursed amulet fell into his possession.

### Session 4
- Rescued from the massacre by the sea elves [[Vespera]] and [[Kaelen]].

### Session 6
- Unknowingly became the target of the [[Sovereign Prophet]]'s ultimatum: [[Icarus]] must deliver the amulet thief within one lunar cycle.

### Session 9
- In a bioluminescent sanctuary, learned the amulet is the [[Shard of the Great Devourer]] and that he is the **Shepherd** — counterpart to the Prophet, [[Kaelen]] and [[Vespera]]'s brother Valyrian. Trained with the sea elves; dreamt of inhabiting the Devourer's body. Failed spectacularly to woo [[Vespera]]. Returned via Fathomless Plunge to impale [[Tlincalli]] with a spectral tentacle.

### Session 10
- Claimed the [[Ring of the Rotten]]; threw [[Rik]] the [[Flame Tongue Whip]]; vaporized the last mummy with Booming Blade.

### Session 11
- Sprinted the Hallway of the Keeper's traps ("Jack-Attack!").

### Session 12
- Suffered a hypothermic seizure in the sudden tundra; recovered at [[Holly Hollow]].

### Session 13
- Still comatose, witnessed an apocalyptic warning from the [[Sovereign Prophet]]. With him conscious again, the party set out north toward [[Hartsvale]].

### Session 14
- **Smooth-talked a massive winter bear** into walking away after it ambushed the party's polar bear mounts on the trek to [[Hartsvale]].
- Like the rest of the party, suffered the [[Baba Yaga]]'s nightmare invasions — waking damage, exhaustion, and intense paranoia.

### Session 15
- **Refused entry to [[Hartsvale]]** at the main gates: the Orc guards barred him (a human) and [[Gearsley]] (a warforged), citing ancient resentment over [[Phandelver's Pact]]. The two stayed behind at an **eastern outpost** while [[Vlad]], [[Rik]], and [[Wild Fury]] entered the city.

### Session 16
- Revealed in the throne room to have been **captured by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]**, along with [[Gearsley]] — the pair having last been seen stranded at the eastern outpost.
- Kholodan was persuaded to release **[[Gearsley]]** to join the expedition, but **kept Blackjack behind as collateral** alongside the chained [[Vlad]] — leaving the Shepherd of [[The Great Devourer]] a hostage in a frost giant's castle while the rest of the party marches on [[The Devil in the Ice]].

### Session 21
- **Rescued from [[Hartsvale]]'s dungeons**, concussed and still bearing the pulsing [[Shard of the Great Devourer]], after [[X the Hamster|X]] gnawed through his iron chains.
- Travelled with the party to [[Brinewater Bay]]. At ***The Codfather*** tavern, X's attempt to cast ***Greater Restoration*** on him **backfired with necrotic damage**, revealing that Blackjack is **willingly acting as the amulet's vessel and accepting [[The Great Devourer|the patron]]**.
- Later taken by X to rest at ***The Codfather Part II*** while the rest of the party explored the brothel.
