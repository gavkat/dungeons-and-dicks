# Immovable Rod

**Tags:** #Item

---

## Overview
A rod that fixes itself immovably in space. Twice saved the party from crushing death in the [[Tomb of Tzentak]] — halting a descending ceiling ([[Session 10]]) and a rolling boulder ([[Session 11]]).

**The third time, it failed.** As the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] came down on [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] ([[Session 18]]), [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] **lunged with the rod** to brace the collapsing stone — and his **footing gave way**. The button clicked and the rod **locked rigidly into empty air inches short of the stone**, **hovering uselessly in the blizzard** while millions of tons of rock sealed his father in the dark. It is presumably still there, fixed in the air over a buried mountain.
