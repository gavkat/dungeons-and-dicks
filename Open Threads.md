## Active Quests

- **The Prophet's ultimatum** — [[Icarus]] has one lunar cycle (from [[Session 6]]) to deliver the amulet thief to the [[Sovereign Prophet]]. The thief is [[Blackjack]]. The clock is ticking.
- **Capture [[The Devil in the Ice]] — alive. FAILED, catastrophically ([[Session 18]]).** [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]) was to collar the Devil with an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** from [[Glimmerspark Industries]], for the party's **freedom**, the **voiding of [[Vlad]]'s execution**, and **Wild Fury II's liberation**. Instead the party **collared [[Vraxis]] by mistake**, then **spent the collar on Wild Fury II** to break his returning bloodlust; the **[[Dragon-Skull Cave]] collapsed**, **Wild Fury II is sealed inside it**, [[Icarus]] was **killed**, and the **Devil was never found**. They owe [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] a monster they do not have, and he still holds [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]].
- **Free [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]]** — both **held in [[Hartsvale]] as collateral** against that errand ([[Session 16]]). Blackjack had been secretly **captured** by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] along with [[Gearsley]]; [[Vlad]] sits **muzzled and chained** with an execution hanging over him.
- **Wild Fury's father — FOUND ([[Session 15]]), FOUGHT and now travelling with the party ([[Session 16]]).** He is **Wild Fury II**, **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**. The reunion was a massacre: spellbound and with **no memory of his son**, he cut [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] down with *"You always disappointed me, son."* **Liberated** under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain, he now marches with them — but **what ended the rage, and whether it can take him again, is unanswered**. **The quest is now emotional, not geographical ([[Session 17]]):** lucid and free, the father **outpaces, dismisses and snaps at** his son, who keeps openly reaching for a validation he does not get. **And then he was gone ([[Session 18]]):** a **tender moment** over the [[Wand of Wonder]], the **bloodlust returning**, the son **collaring his own father** to break it — and at the collapsing dragon-skull threshold, the father **tackling his son clear of a falling monolith**, **catching the mountain on his own back**, ordering him to run, and saying that after fighting for other men **he would die on his own terms**. The [[Immovable Rod]] locked **inches short**. He was **sealed in the dark** and **not seen to die**. (Earlier leads: last seen near the [[Ice Spires]] via [[Halia]]; possibly the "missing guildmate," [[Session 5]].)
- **Dig out Wild Fury II ([[Session 18]]).** He braced the collapsing stone jaws of the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] bare-pawed so his son could run, wearing [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**, and **millions of tons of rock sealed him in the dark**. Nobody saw him die. The [[Immovable Rod]] is presumably still fixed in the air above the rubble.
- **What happened to [[Icarus]]? ([[Session 18]])** [[Vraxis]] **stopped his heart with lightning** and **hurled his body into the abyss**. He has died twice before and come back both times, and the Feywild reportedly holds him an **immortal god** — but the source says nothing about what followed.
- ~~**[[The White Claw|The Krug]] death match**~~ — **RESOLVED, badly ([[Session 16]]).** [[Wild Fury]] **lost.** [[Vlad]]'s loophole required him to *defeat* his father; instead the champion decapitated [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey]], dropped [[Icarus]] and [[Rik]], and axed Wild Fury III through the chest. **[[Vlad]]'s exile stands**, and his execution is now voidable only through [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s new bargain.
- ~~**Free [[Icarus]]**~~ — **RESOLVED ([[Session 16]]).** Out of the box and back in the fight: he took **Giant Ape** form against the champion, unleashed a burst of magical energy, and was knocked unconscious. *(What [[Kenky]] — or [[Reginald]] — wanted with him is still open; see Mysteries.)*
- **Rescue [[Dave]]** — [[Wild Fury]]'s wyvern was abducted by [[The Ashen Figure]], a [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black," on the road north ([[Session 13]]). Where was he taken, and why?
- **The five remaining [[Vox of the Dark Six|Voxes]]** — destroying [[Tzentak]] (a Vox) froze the world; five more avatars of [[The Dark Six]] now weave a net of darkness across the continent and hunt the party ([[Session 13]]).
- **Reunite the party** — ~~split three ways~~ ~~mostly back together ([[Session 16]])~~ — **broken apart again ([[Session 18]])**: **Wild Fury II** is **buried under the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]**, **[[Icarus]]** was **killed by [[Vraxis]]** and thrown into an abyss, and **[[Blackjack]]** is still hostage in [[Hartsvale]]. [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] was tackled clear into the snow; the rest of the party was racing for the same threshold as the mountain came down.
- **Marisya's cure** — the [[Anauroch]]/[[Tomb of Tzentak]] lead is spent and [[Tzentak]] destroyed. Was a cure found? [[Rik]]'s quest stands unresolved.
- **Recover what the thief took** — ~~an unseen thief~~ **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]] ([[Session 18]])**, caught playing with the party's gear in [[Vraxis]]'s archive. **[[Gearsley]]'s gun came back** (the tyke holding it shot itself). **Nothing has been said about the gold, the rations, or [[Rik]]'s [[Flame Tongue Whip]]** — and the cave they were in is now a collapsed mountain.

## Mysteries

- **What is [[Gearsley]]?** Revealed to be a **warforged** ([[Session 15]], when [[Hartsvale]]'s Orc guards barred him from the city) — but that still doesn't explain how he revived a dead man ([[Session 1]]).
- **Who is [[Reginald]]?** — [[Icarus]]'s father, and (like Icarus) an **Aarakocra**. His **Kenku** followers are present in [[Hartsvale]] ([[Session 15]]). Why are they there, and what does he want?
- **[[Kenky]]'s betrayal** — a Kenku childhood friend of [[Icarus]] who knew his true identity and trapped him in a metal box ([[Session 15]]). On his own initiative, or on someone's orders?
- **The Tethered** — what is the Forge of Spells doing to people? Black-eyed transformations at [[Leilon]] ([[Session 1]]); see [[The Tethered]].
- **"Pieces of six" / What is [[Polly]]?** — the whisper became a sapphire-eyed chant of *"Pieces of Six!"* ([[Session 13]]), increasingly tied to the six [[Vox of the Dark Six|Voxes]] of [[The Dark Six]] — who **now have names** ([[Session 16]]: The Mischief, [[The Keeper]], [[The Great Devourer|The Devourer]], The Fury, [[The Traveler]], The Shadow). And [[Vraxis]] has sensed Polly is **no mere bird, but a mysterious fey entity** ([[Session 14]]). In [[Session 17]] the Feywild flumph **[[Phlumphi]]** recognized her on [[Icarus]]'s shoulder, said **the two had successfully found each other**, and vanished to **report the news back** — so the pairing was *expected*, and somebody in the fae realm is waiting to hear about it.
- **The [[Baba Yaga]]** — what is the dream-entity haunting the north's nights, dealing waking damage, exhaustion, and paranoia — and driving victims to self-mutilation ([[Session 14]])? It struck the dying party as a **single shared nightmare** in [[Session 16]] — and [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]] now blames [[Hartsvale]]'s nightmares on **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** instead. Wrong, lying, or is the Devil the Baba Yaga? **[[Session 18]] gives a third answer:** [[Vraxis]], who lives in the mountain, says the mountain **merely echoes nightmares bred within Kholodan's own castle** — pointing the whole haunting back at [[Hartsvale]]. The party, meanwhile, has started using "the devil in the ice" and "Baba Yaga" as **the same name**.
- **What is "[[The Devil in the Ice]]"?** — the thing in the dragon-skull cave that [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] wants **caged, not killed** ([[Session 16]]). Nothing of its nature is known, and the party **collapsed the cave without ever meeting it** ([[Session 18]]). What is known now: **[[Archibald]] came hunting it years earlier** in the belief it could **cure his brothers**, and **[[Vraxis]] scoffed at being mistaken for it**. Why does a frost giant lord need it alive badly enough to trade away his own brother's execution — and why did a goblin think it could heal?
- **Why did [[Princess Zaria|Princess Zarya]] save the party — in secret?** ([[Session 16]]) The Twilight Maiden pulled the [[Boner Bros]] back from death after **her own champion** killed them, and did it quietly. Whose side is she on?
- **What is Wild Fury II's rage, and why did it come back?** A **supernatural, blinding rage** seized him on arriving in [[Hartsvale]] five years ago and stopped him leaving ([[Session 16]]); [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] could aim it at his own son. It **surged again** in [[Vraxis]]'s archive ([[Session 18]]) — three days' march from Hartsvale, with the lord nowhere near — and what broke it that time was an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]**, which means it answers to **arcane suppression**. Nobody has said what it is or who is casting it.
- **Where is [[Dew Dew Bun]] — and the [[Elven Lute]]?** The fairy bard vanished six years ago and the lute that powered her ([[Tymora]]'s gift to [[Beshaba]]) has never been accounted for ([[Session 16]]).
- **Who are The Fury and The Shadow?** — two of the six [[The Dark Six|Dark Six]] titles Wild Fury II recited ([[Session 16]]) with no face attached to either.
- ~~**Who were the shadowy figures in the snow?**~~ — **ANSWERED ([[Session 17]]):** **[[Ice Golems|ice golems]]**, ambushing out of an avalanche on the slopes below the cave. *Who or what made them, and what they were guarding, is still open.*
- ~~**Who is the unseen thief?**~~ — **ANSWERED ([[Session 18]]):** **[[Yeti-Tykes|yeti-tykes]]** — juvenile yetis denning in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]], who got past a warforged's alert watch and were later found playing with the loot. Not the Devil, not a cult; children.
- ~~**Who petrified the yeti at the campsite?**~~ — **ANSWERED ([[Session 18]]):** the goblin adventurer **[[Archibald]]**, with a **stolen wand**, per the letter found on his frozen corpse.
- **Who was [[Archibald]] hunting for, and who is Icey?** — the frozen goblin came after **[[The Devil in the Ice]]** to **cure his brothers' afflictions**, and left a letter to his **brother Icey** ([[Session 18]]). What the afflictions are, how a caged Devil was meant to cure them, and whether this Icey is the **Goblin gladiator [[Krug Gladiators|Icey]]** who died in the Krug ([[Session 15]]) are all unstated.
- **Who is [[The Cloaked Figure|the cloaked figure]] on the astral plane?** — it caught [[Icarus]] mid-displacement, **warned him of his reckless chaos**, and commanded ***"Fly, boy. Fly."*** ([[Session 18]]). Third party in a row to flag his chaos, after [[Vraxis]] ([[Session 14]]) and, obliquely, [[Phlumphi]] ([[Session 17]]). Is it who Phlumphi was reporting to?
- **What is [[Vraxis]] doing under the mountain?** — a **cobalt dragon soldier** who keeps a **lavish subterranean archive** below a dragon-skull cave, cooks Cajun sausages, and knows enough about [[Hartsvale]] to say the nightmares are bred in [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s castle ([[Session 18]]). Archive of *what*? And did he get out of his own cave-in?
- **Where did the crooked [[Wand of Wonder]] come from?** — [[The White Claw (Champion)|Wild Fury II]] knew it as a relic **once wielded by a drunken townsfolk wizard** ([[Session 18]]). Which townsfolk, which town, and how did it reach a goblin dead in the [[Spine of the World]]?
- **Why do [[Rik]]'s [[Wyrmreaver Gauntlets]] match the mural?** — the runes in their palm are an **exact match** for the ancient dragon rune at the center of the **five-headed dragon matriarch** carving in the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]] ([[Session 17]]) — and pressing them to the mural **unsealed a hidden marble stairway** into the cave's lower levels ([[Session 18]]). They are a **key**. Where did Rik get them, who cut a door that only they open, and what is the rune for?
- **Who is the five-headed dragon matriarch?** — the terrifying mural the [[Dragon-Skull Cave]]'s carvings of a **primeval giant–dragon war** build toward ([[Session 17]]), and the point where the path forks. Unnamed so far.
- **Why does the fae realm celebrate [[Icarus]] as an immortal god?** — [[Phlumphi]] said so plainly ([[Session 17]]), and left to **report back** that Icarus and [[Polly]] had found each other. Report back *to whom*?
- **Where did the ice titan go?** — [[Icarus]] **banished** the colossal titan **to another plane of existence** rather than killing it ([[Session 17]]). Banishments in this campaign have not always been permanent.
- ****Gradley**** — [[Icarus]]'s reincarnated body belongs to [[Garvin]]'s lost brother ([[Session 14]]). Who was Gradley, how did his body end up in the [[Itju-Sha]] refuse heap — and what happens when Garvin learns the truth?

- **Who is [[The Ashen Figure]]?** — the ash-cloaked, portal-stepping [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black" who took [[Dave]]. Connected to [[The Traveler]] or the [[Vox of the Dark Six|Voxes]]?
- **What is [[The Consortium]]?** — the power behind the [[Mage Guard]], with "Code Black" assets and a chain of command. Tied to [[Phandelver]] / [[Glimmerspark Industries]] — whose motto, *"One World. One Voice."*, was first seen on its arctic convoys ([[Session 14]])?
