## Active Quests

- **The Prophet's ultimatum** — [[Icarus]] has one lunar cycle (from [[Session 6]]) to deliver the amulet thief to the [[Sovereign Prophet]]. The thief is [[Blackjack]]. The clock is ticking.
- **Capture [[The Devil in the Ice]] — alive.** [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Lord Kholodan]]'s bargain ([[Session 16]]): enter a **dragon-skull cave** in the **[[Spine of the World]]** and collar the Devil with an **[[Arcane Dampener Collar]]** from [[Glimmerspark Industries]]. The prize is the party's **freedom**, the **voiding of [[Vlad]]'s execution**, and **Wild Fury II's liberation**. They reached the cliff-face and were met by a **blizzard, an avalanching frost cloud, and shadowy figures charging through the snow**.
- **Free [[Blackjack]] and [[Vlad]]** — both **held in [[Hartsvale]] as collateral** against that errand ([[Session 16]]). Blackjack had been secretly **captured** by [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]] along with [[Gearsley]]; [[Vlad]] sits **muzzled and chained** with an execution hanging over him.
- **Wild Fury's father — FOUND ([[Session 15]]), FOUGHT and now travelling with the party ([[Session 16]]).** He is **Wild Fury II**, **"[[The White Claw (Champion)|The White Claw]]"**. The reunion was a massacre: spellbound and with **no memory of his son**, he cut [[Wild Fury|Wild Fury III]] down with *"You always disappointed me, son."* **Liberated** under [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s bargain, he now marches with them — but **what ended the rage, and whether it can take him again, is unanswered**. (Earlier leads: last seen near the [[Ice Spires]] via [[Halia]]; possibly the "missing guildmate," [[Session 5]].)
- ~~**[[The White Claw|The Krug]] death match**~~ — **RESOLVED, badly ([[Session 16]]).** [[Wild Fury]] **lost.** [[Vlad]]'s loophole required him to *defeat* his father; instead the champion decapitated [[Krug Gladiators|Humphrey]], dropped [[Icarus]] and [[Rik]], and axed Wild Fury III through the chest. **[[Vlad]]'s exile stands**, and his execution is now voidable only through [[Lord Kholodan Ghartovich|Kholodan]]'s new bargain.
- ~~**Free [[Icarus]]**~~ — **RESOLVED ([[Session 16]]).** Out of the box and back in the fight: he took **Giant Ape** form against the champion, unleashed a burst of magical energy, and was knocked unconscious. *(What [[Kenky]] — or [[Reginald]] — wanted with him is still open; see Mysteries.)*
- **Rescue [[Dave]]** — [[Wild Fury]]'s wyvern was abducted by [[The Ashen Figure]], a [[The Consortium|Consortium]] "Code Black," on the road north ([[Session 13]]). Where was he taken, and why?
- **The five remaining [[Vox of the Dark Six|Voxes]]** — destroying [[Tzentak]] (a Vox) froze the world; five more avatars of [[The Dark Six]] now weave a net of darkness across the continent and hunt the party ([[Session 13]]).
- **Reunite the party** — ~~split three ways~~ **mostly back together ([[Session 16]])**: [[Wild Fury]], [[Rik]], [[Icarus]] and [[Gearsley]] march on the [[Spine of the World]] with **Wild Fury II**. Only **[[Blackjack]]** is still missing from the group — held hostage in [[Hartsvale]].
- **Marisya's cure** — the [[Anauroch]]/[[Tomb of Tzentak]] lead is spent and [[Tzentak]] destroyed. Was a cure found? [[Rik]]'s quest stands unresolved.
